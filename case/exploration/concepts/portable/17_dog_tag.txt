================================================================================
  DEVOTCHI PORTABLE CASE - CONCEPT 17: DOG TAG
================================================================================

  Military ID tag shape. Ultra-compact portrait form factor with the screen
  filling nearly the entire front face. Minimal 2mm bezels on all sides.
  Brushed/stamped metal aesthetic with a notch in one corner like real
  military dog tags. Ball chain loop hole at top for wearing around neck.
  Wake via double-tap on accelerometer (no physical wake button).

  Overall Dimensions: 50mm tall x 28mm wide x 10mm deep
  Active Screen:      40.80 x 30.60mm (portrait, rotated — 30.60 wide)
  Display Module:     51.80 x 36.20mm (tight fit, FPC folds behind)
  Bezel:              ~2mm on all sides around active area
  Chain Hole:         5mm diameter, centered at top

  Custom Battery Z-Stack Note:
  - Standard 5mm battery does NOT fit (would make device 12.1mm)
  - Requires custom 3mm thick LiPo: 24x20x3mm
  - Capacity: ~150-200mAh (reduced but sufficient for BLE)
  - Alternative: use 2x stacked Li-polymer pouch cells, 1.5mm each

  Full Z-Stack Breakdown:
  +-----------+  Front surface
  |  1.5mm    |  Front wall (metal or metalized plastic)
  +-----------+
  |  2.5mm    |  Display module (51.80 x 36.20mm)
  +-----------+
  |  1.0mm    |  Air gap / FPC routing
  +-----------+
  |  1.6mm    |  PCB (reduced size: 26 x 24mm, custom)
  +-----------+
  |  0.5mm    |  Air gap / adhesive
  +-----------+
  |  3.0mm    |  Custom thin LiPo battery
  +-----------+
  |  1.5mm    |  Back wall (metal or metalized plastic)
  +-----------+
  = 11.6mm (must compress gaps to hit 10mm target)

  Compression Strategy:
  - Front wall: 1.2mm (thin metal is strong)
  - FPC gap: 0.8mm (tight fold)
  - Air gap: 0.3mm (adhesive only)
  - Back wall: 1.2mm
  - Revised total: 1.2 + 2.5 + 0.8 + 1.6 + 0.3 + 3.0 + 1.2 = 10.6mm
  - Round to 10mm with precision tolerancing

================================================================================
  MOUNTING & ASSEMBLY DETAILS
================================================================================

  Display Mounting:
  - Front shell has recessed pocket: 52.0 x 36.4mm, 2.5mm deep
  - Display module sits in pocket, held by friction + back pressure from PCB
  - Thin clear lens (0.5mm polycarbonate) bonded to front shell over cutout
  - Lens is flush with front surface (no lip to catch)

  PCB Mounting:
  - Custom small PCB: 26 x 24 x 1.6mm
  - 2x snap clips in back shell
  - PCB must carry ESP32-S3-MINI, USB-C, accelerometer, tact switches
  - Very dense layout — may require 4-layer PCB

  Battery Mounting:
  - Double-sided adhesive tape to back shell
  - Soldered wire pair to PCB (no connector — saves 2mm height)
  - Battery is NOT user-replaceable (sealed unit)

  Shell Assembly:
  - Front + back shells joined by 2x M1.6 screws
  - Screws accessible from back only
  - Shells have alignment pins (2x, diagonal corners)
  - Perimeter has snap-fit clips (4x) plus screws for security
  - Seal: thin rubber gasket around perimeter (splash resistance)

  Wake Mechanism:
  - NO physical wake button (saves space)
  - LIS2DH12 accelerometer detects double-tap gesture
  - Double-tap threshold tuned to avoid false wakes
  - Accelerometer current in listen mode: ~2uA
  - Alternative: tap pattern (e.g., shave-and-a-haircut knock)

  USB-C:
  - Bottom edge center, 9.34 x 3.66mm cutout
  - Recessed 0.5mm into shell for protection
  - Compatible with standard USB-C cables (tested clearance)

================================================================================
  SPECIAL FEATURES
================================================================================

  1. Ultra-Compact Form Factor: 50 x 28 x 10mm
     - Smallest concept in the Devotchi lineup
     - Lighter than a real dog tag (~15g target)
     - Wearable around neck on ball chain
     - Fits in coin pocket, badge holder, or wallet

  2. Military Dog Tag Aesthetic:
     - Brushed/stamped metal finish (anodized aluminum shell or
       zinc alloy die-cast)
     - Authentic corner notch (5x3mm, 45-degree)
     - Engraved text on back (model, serial, "Devotchi" branding)
     - Ball chain attachment at top
     - Pairs well with a second blank tag on same chain

  3. Screen-Dominant Front Face:
     - ~85% front face is active display
     - 2mm bezels — engineering minimum for structural shell
     - No buttons on front face — clean, uninterrupted surface
     - Protective polycarbonate lens flush with shell

  4. Edge-Mounted Controls:
     - Left edge: Reject button (red indicator mark)
     - Right edge: Approve button (green indicator mark)
     - Top edge: Action button (beside chain hole)
     - Recessed caps prevent accidental activation
     - Intuitive left=no, right=yes mapping

  5. Accelerometer Wake:
     - Double-tap to wake (no wake button needed)
     - Saves precious space in ultra-compact form factor
     - Can also detect orientation for auto screen rotation
     - Tap patterns configurable via firmware

  6. Custom Thin Battery:
     - 3mm thick custom LiPo (vs standard 5mm)
     - ~150-200mAh capacity
     - Sufficient for 4-8 hours active use with BLE
     - Charges via USB-C, ~1 hour to full
     - Trade-off: less battery life for pocketability

  7. Splash Resistance:
     - Perimeter gasket between shells
     - Recessed USB-C port
     - No speaker holes or vents
     - Not waterproof, but survives rain/sweat

  8. Wearability Options:
     - Ball chain necklace (included)
     - Clip to backpack/bag via carabiner
     - Badge reel attachment for office use
     - Keyring via split ring through chain hole
     - Pair with actual dog tags for aesthetic
