DESIGN 5: RETRO TV
==================
Chunky CRT television shape with rounded front bezel, antenna stubs,
and knob-style buttons on the side. Screen centered in front face.
Think "1970s portable TV" miniaturized.

Overall: 165 x 110 x 65mm (landscape, chunky box)
Target user: nostalgic desk ornament, conversation piece

COMPONENT LAYOUT
────────────────

    FRONT SHELL (CRT-style body)
    ├── Rounded front face with screen bezel
    │   ├── Screen window (41.8 x 31.6mm, radiused corners)
    │   ├── Screen pocket (52.2 x 36.6mm, 5mm deep)
    │   ├── 4x retention clips
    │   └── Bezel overlap: 5mm around window
    ├── Right side wall: 3x button holes (8mm dia for knob caps)
    ├── Left side wall: speaker grille (28mm dia pattern)
    ├── 2x antenna stubs on top (decorative, 6mm dia, 25mm tall)
    ├── LED hole on front face below screen
    └── Wall: 2–3mm (thicker at front for sculpted profile)

    BACK SHELL (flat back plate)
    ├── Vent slot array (2 x 15mm slots, 3 rows)
    ├── USB-C cutout centered (9.5 x 3.5mm)
    ├── 4x screw bosses
    ├── 4x PCB standoffs (5mm OD, M2, 8mm tall)
    └── 2x feet/pads on bottom edge

    PCB
    ├── Mounted to back shell standoffs
    ├── ESP32 faces back (antenna toward vent slots for WiFi)
    ├── USB-C on back edge
    ├── Buttons on right edge (wire leads to side-mounted tactile switches)
    └── Screen FPC routes up and over to front pocket

    SPEAKER
    ├── 28mm diameter, mounted to left side wall
    ├── Foam gasket seal against inner wall
    └── Fires out through left side grille


ANTENNA DETAIL
──────────────

    ┌─ Antenna stub ─┐
    │  6mm dia        │  25mm tall
    │  cylinder       │  Solid decorative piece
    │  (or tapered)   │  Press-fit into top shell sockets
    └─────────────────┘

    Can be:
    - Solid cosmetic (just decoration)
    - Functional WiFi antenna extension (hollow, with wire to ESP32 antenna pad)


ASSEMBLY
────────
- 2-piece: front body + back plate, 4x M2 screws from back
- Antenna stubs: press-fit into sockets in top of front shell
- Button knobs: separate caps that press onto tactile switch stems
- Screen loads from inside front shell before closing
- Speaker glued/press-fit to inner left wall with foam gasket
