DESCRIPTION
───────────
Adjustable gooseneck desk lamp form factor. The 5.0" screen is housed in a
compact head piece at the top of a flexible metal gooseneck tube. The
gooseneck allows infinite angle adjustment — tilt, rotate, raise, lower.
A weighted circular base contains the speaker, three buttons in a row on
top, and the USB-C port in back. The screen head has a slight hood/shade
protruding over the top edge, evoking a real desk lamp. Internal wiring
runs through the gooseneck center. A steel disc in the base provides
stability against the leverage of the extended gooseneck.

OVERALL DIMENSIONS
──────────────────
  Head:       65 × 55 × 20 mm (W × H × D at thickest)
  Gooseneck:  8 mm diameter, 150 mm long
  Base:       70 mm diameter × 20 mm tall
  Wall:       2.0 mm (head and base shells)
  Total max height: ~190 mm (gooseneck fully vertical)

MOUNTING DETAILS
────────────────

  Screen Mount (in head):
    - Landscape, 120×75mm module fills head cavity
    - 4 internal clip tabs grip module edges
    - Active area visible through front bezel opening
    - Back plate secured with 2 × M2 screws
    - Head shell: 2-part (front bezel + back plate)

  Gooseneck Connection:
    - Bottom of head: threaded brass insert (M8 or press-fit)
    - Top of base: threaded brass insert (M8 or press-fit)
    - Gooseneck tube threads into both ends
    - Alternative: press-fit with adhesive + set screw
    - Cable passes through tube center (6mm ID)

  PCB Mount (in base):
    - Custom circular PCB recommended (55mm dia)
    - Or: standard 110×65mm PCB trimmed to fit
    - Mounted on 3 × M2 standoffs (3mm height)
    - Component side up (buttons and speaker face up)

  Buttons:
    - 3 × 6mm tactile switches on PCB
    - Through-hole in base top surface
    - Custom flat keycaps flush with surface
    - 15mm center-to-center spacing
    - Arranged in arc following base curvature

  Speaker:
    - 28mm dia × 3.5mm on PCB, faces up
    - Sound exits through vent holes in base top
    - Vent pattern: ring of small holes around button area
    - Sealed below by steel disc (acts as baffle)

  USB-C:
    - Cutout in base side wall (rear-facing)
    - 8.94 × 3.26 mm opening
    - 5mm from base bottom edge
    - Strain relief clip inside

  Counterweight:
    - Steel disc: 60mm dia × 3-5mm thick (~120g)
    - Press-fit into base bottom cavity
    - Critical: must offset gooseneck + head weight
    - Base must not tip when gooseneck extended horizontally


ASSEMBLY NOTES
──────────────

  1. Press steel counterweight disc into base bottom cavity
  2. Apply rubber ring/feet to base bottom
  3. Mount PCB onto base standoffs (3 × M2 screws)
  4. Solder/connect speaker to PCB
  5. Route USB-C extension to base side cutout
  6. Thread gooseneck tube into base brass insert
  7. Route 4-wire cable through gooseneck tube
  8. Thread gooseneck tube into head brass insert
  9. Connect cable to screen FPC/connector inside head
  10. Seat screen module into head bezel clips
  11. Attach head back plate (2 × M2 screws)

  Head shell: 2-part (front bezel + back plate)
  Base shell: 2-part (top dome + bottom plate)
  Gooseneck: purchased part (standard chrome/black flexible tube)
  Material: PLA/PETG for shells, chrome gooseneck tube
  Print orientation: Head front face-down, base halves flat


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

  Infinite Angle Adjustment:
    - Gooseneck maintains any position set by user
    - Tilt screen toward face, away, sideways — any angle
    - Raise high for standing desk, lower for sitting
    - Twist to switch between landscape orientations
    - No locks or detents needed — friction holds position

  Hood/Shade:
    - 5mm overhang on top edge of screen head
    - Reduces overhead light glare on screen
    - Evokes desk lamp aesthetic
    - Can be extended to 10mm for more shade effect
    - Slight downward curve on leading edge

  Cable Management:
    - All wiring hidden inside gooseneck tube
    - Only USB-C cable visible (exits base back)
    - Clean single-cable desk footprint
    - Internal cable has slack for full gooseneck range

  Weighted Base Stability:
    - 120g steel disc counterbalances ~50g head + gooseneck
    - Base stays planted even at extreme gooseneck angles
    - Rubber ring base prevents sliding
    - Low center of gravity design

  Compact Base Controls:
    - Buttons on base top — always accessible
    - Speaker also in base — sound comes from desk level
    - All electronics in base — head is just screen
    - Minimizes head weight for better gooseneck behavior

  Desk Lamp Illusion:
    - From across a room, looks like a small desk lamp
    - Screen "glow" enhances the lamp metaphor
    - Hood/shade sells the silhouette
    - Conversation starter: "Is that a lamp or a screen?"
