DESIGN 12: FLIP PHONE
=====================
2000s clamshell phone homage. Tall narrow portrait form factor.
Flip open reveals screen on upper half with T9-style button grid on
lower half. Small outer status window (LED hole). Spring-loaded hinge
with 160-degree detent. Decorative antenna nub at top corner.
When closed the two halves stack to a compact pocket-friendly brick.

Overall open:   110mm tall x 48mm wide x 12mm deep (each half ~10mm)
Overall closed:  55mm tall x 48mm wide x 22mm deep
Weight target: ~52g with battery
Orientation: Portrait
Shell: 4-piece (upper front, upper back, lower front, lower back) + hinge

─────────────

    Cross-section through hinge barrel:

              Upper half
         ┌────────────────┐
         │                │

              passes
         ┌── through ────┐
         │   ╔═══════╗   │ ← hinge barrel (6mm outer dia)
         │   ║ spring ║   │   spring-loaded detent
         │   ║ ┌───┐  ║   │   clicks at 0° (closed) and 160° (open)
         │   ║ │FPC│  ║   │   FPC cable runs through hollow core
         │   ║ └───┘  ║   │   (1.5mm wide channel)
         │   ╚═══════╝   │
         └── ─── ─── ────┘
              passes
    ─────┐   through     ┌─────
         │                │
         └────────────────┘
              Lower half

    FPC cable path:
    Display FPC → routes down upper half → through hinge barrel center →
    into lower half → connects to PCB FPC connector

    Hinge specs:
    - 6mm outer diameter barrel
    - 2mm hollow core for FPC pass-through
    - Spring-loaded ball detent (2 positions: 0° closed, 160° open)
    - Metal pin (2mm dia stainless steel) through both sides
    - Barrel extends full 48mm width for rigidity
    - FPC: 0.5mm pitch, 12-pin, rated for 50K flex cycles

    HINGE ASSEMBLY
    ├── Stainless steel pin (2mm dia, 52mm long)
    ├── Barrel halves molded into upper/lower shells
    ├── Spring + ball detent mechanism (0° and 160°)

    LOWER HALF — Layer 1: FRONT SHELL (55 x 48mm, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── 4x button holes (2x2 grid, 12.5 x 6.5mm each)

    LOWER HALF — Layer 2: PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5mm) faces back

    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge (centered)
    ├── Display FPC connector on top edge (near hinge)
    ├── Vibration motor pads near bottom
    └── Mounted on 4x posts from lower back shell

    LOWER HALF — Layer 3: 0.5mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module height

    LOWER HALF — Layer 4: BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo, 300-500mAh)

    ├── JST connector to PCB (short pigtail)

    LOWER HALF — Layer 5: BACK SHELL (55 x 48mm, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── 4x PCB mounting posts (3mm OD, press-fit)

    ├── Battery door (snap-off panel, 32 x 37mm)
    ├── Hinge barrel socket (left and right sides)
    ├── USB-C cutout (9.5 x 4.0mm)
    └── Vibration motor pocket (7mm dia, foam tape)


MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- 4-piece shell: upper front, upper back, lower front, lower back
- Hinge pin inserted last — locks upper and lower halves together
- Each half uses 2x snap-fit tabs + 2x registration pins (no screws)
- FPC cable must be pre-routed through hinge barrel before pin insertion
- FPC rated for 50K open/close cycles minimum
- Display press-fits into upper front shell pocket
- PCB press-fits onto 4x posts in lower back shell
- Battery sits in cradle ribs in lower back shell
- USB-C cutout at bottom of lower half only
- Vibration motor adhered inside lower back shell with foam tape
- LED hole in upper back shell — visible when phone is closed
- Antenna nub: cosmetic, molded as part of upper back shell
- Closed dimensions: very pocket-friendly at 55 x 48 x 22mm
- Hinge rated for 20K open/close cycles minimum
- Spring detent provides satisfying click at open and closed positions
- FPC flex radius inside hinge barrel: minimum 2mm bend radius


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Satisfying flip-open action with spring detent at 160°
- Outer LED visible when closed for status at a glance
- Decorative antenna nub for authentic retro feel
- T9-style 2x2 button grid references classic phone keypads
- Compact closed form (55 x 48 x 22mm) fits any pocket
- FPC pass-through hinge allows single PCB design
- Closing the phone could trigger sleep mode (hall sensor + magnet)
