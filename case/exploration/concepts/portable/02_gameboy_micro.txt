DESIGN 2: GAMEBOY MICRO
=======================
Landscape orientation rectangular handheld. Screen centered with
buttons on either side. Comfortable two-hand hold like a tiny game
controller. Think "GameBoy Micro" or "Analogue Pocket" miniaturized.

Overall: 95mm wide x 55mm tall x 16mm deep
Weight target: ~50g with battery
Orientation: Landscape
Corner radius: 5mm
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), 4x M2 screws

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, front to back)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT SHELL (1.5mm wall, 5mm corner radius)
    ├── Screen window cutout (41.8 x 31.6mm, landscape, centered)
    ├── 4x button holes (12.5 x 6.5mm, 2 left + 2 right)
    ├── 1x LED hole (2mm dia, bottom-right of screen area)
    ├── 4x screen retention clips (L-shaped, 0.5mm overhang)
    ├── Grip texture zones: 2x recessed areas (15 x 25mm)
    │   with 0.5mm deep cross-hatch pattern on left/right edges
    └── 4x M2 threaded inserts (heat-set, brass)

    Layer 2 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Landscape orientation (native, no rotation needed)
    ├── Seated in pocket with 0.2mm clearance per side
    ├── FPC cable exits bottom edge
    └── Held by front shell retention clips

    Layer 3 — 2.5mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC cable routes to PCB connector
    └── Button plungers pass through

    Layer 4 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5mm) faces back
    ├── 4x tactile switches face front (2 left, 2 right of center)
    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge (centered)
    ├── Display FPC connector on top edge
    ├── Vibration motor pads (bottom-left area)
    ├── Optional piezo speaker pads (center-back area)
    └── Mounted on 4x M2 standoffs (5mm OD, 2.5mm tall)

    Layer 5 — 0.5mm GAP + BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo)
    ├── Battery offset to one side of ESP32 module
    ├── JST connector pigtail to PCB
    └── Foam tape pad on back shell for retention

    Layer 6 — BACK SHELL (1.5mm wall, 5mm corner radius)
    ├── 4x M2 screw pass-through holes (countersunk)
    ├── 4x standoff bosses (5mm OD, mate with PCB standoffs)
    ├── Battery retention foam pad area
    ├── USB-C cutout (9.5 x 4.0mm)
    └── Optional speaker grille (15 x 8mm, 1mm holes)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece rectangle (front + back), 4x M2 screws from back
- Front shell has heat-set brass threaded inserts (M2 x 3mm)
- Display press-fits into front shell pocket, held by retention clips
- PCB sits on 4x standoffs molded into back shell
- Battery secured with double-sided foam tape to back shell
- USB-C cutout spans both shell halves at bottom center
- Grip texture molded into front shell (no separate grip pads)
- 5mm corner radius on all 4 corners, consistent front and back
- Comfortable two-hand hold: index fingers rest on top edge,
  thumbs reach buttons naturally on left and right
- Speaker grille on back is optional — can be solid panel instead
- Total depth 16mm allows 0.9mm extra for standoff bosses and tolerance
