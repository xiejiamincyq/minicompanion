================================================================================
  DEVOTCHI PORTABLE CASE - CONCEPT 07: ZIPPO / FLIP-LID
================================================================================

  Inspired by a Zippo lighter. Ultra-compact with hinged lid that flips open
  180 degrees to reveal the screen. When closed, lid protects screen and shows
  a decorative front plate. Very pocket-friendly.

  Overall Dimensions: 65mm tall x 45mm wide x 18mm deep
  Lid Dimensions:     ~35mm tall x 45mm wide x 3mm thick (when open)

  HINGE DETAIL (Top-down cross-section at hinge axis)
================================================================================

              Lid                Body

  Hinge Options:
  A) Piano hinge: continuous pin across full width (40mm)
     - Most durable, smooth action
     - Stainless steel pin, 1.5mm dia
     - Molded knuckles in both shells

  B) Dual-post hinge: two hinge posts at edges
     - Simpler tooling
     - Posts: 3mm dia, 5mm long
     - Spring detent for open/closed positions

  Recommended: Option B (dual-post) for simplicity
  - Left and right hinge posts
  - Internal torsion spring for snap-open action
  - Detent positions at 0 deg (closed) and 180 deg (open)

================================================================================
  MOUNTING & ASSEMBLY DETAILS
================================================================================

  Display Mounting:
  - Recessed pocket in front shell: 52.0 x 36.4mm, 2.5mm deep
  - Thin foam frame around display for shock absorption
  - Display pressed in from inside, held by PCB standoffs

  PCB Mounting:
  - 4x snap-in clips molded into body (no screws for PCB)
  - PCB slides in from bottom, clips engage at top
  - This allows compact assembly in tight enclosure

  Battery Mounting:
  - Foam tape to back shell
  - JST connector to PCB, tucked alongside battery

  Button Mounting:
  - Located below lid line (always accessible)
  - Caps: 12 x 6mm, standard profile
  - Tactile switches on PCB, caps press-fit in shell

  Wake Button:
  - Right side, ~30mm from top
  - Small rocker cap: 10 x 4.5mm
  - Recessed to prevent accidental press in pocket

  Hinge Assembly:
  - Hinge posts molded into front shell and lid
  - Stainless steel pin pressed through both
  - Torsion spring wrapped around pin
  - Assembly: insert spring, align lid, press pin through

  Shell Assembly:
  - Front and back shells clip together
  - 2x M2 screws at bottom for security
  - Lid attaches to front shell via hinge before final assembly

  USB-C:
  - Bottom center, 9.34 x 3.66mm cutout
  - Accessible whether lid is open or closed

================================================================================
  SPECIAL FEATURES
================================================================================

  1. Flip Lid: Protects screen when closed, satisfying snap-open action
     - Over-center spring detent holds lid firmly open or closed
     - Inner lid lining (felt/rubber) prevents screen scratches
     - Decorative outer plate: brushed metal look

  2. Ultra-Compact: 65 x 45 x 18mm fits in any pocket
     - Similar size to actual Zippo lighter
     - One-hand operable (flip open with thumb)

  3. Decorative Front Plate Options:
     - Brushed aluminum insert with etched Devotchi logo
     - Or: textured plastic with metallic paint
     - Or: clear window showing screen even when closed (defeats purpose
       but looks cool)

  4. Pocket-Friendly: No protrusions, rounded edges
     - Smooth sides, no belt clip
     - Can stand upright on bottom edge (USB-C acts as base notch)

  5. Satisfying Mechanics:
     - Lid snap-open has tactile + audible click
     - Reminiscent of Zippo opening sound
     - Vibration motor enhances the physical feel

  6. Open/Closed Detection:
     - Small magnet in lid + Hall effect sensor on PCB
     - Screen turns off when lid closed (power saving)
     - Wake on lid open (instant-on feel)
