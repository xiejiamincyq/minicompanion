================================================================================
  DEVOTCHI PORTABLE CASE - CONCEPT 06: PAGER / BEEPER
================================================================================

  90s pager aesthetic. Portrait orientation with flat top and slight wedge
  taper (thicker at bottom). Belt clip integrated into back shell.
  Screen in upper portion, 3 beveled buttons below, recessed side wake button.

  Overall Dimensions: 80mm tall x 55mm wide x 18mm deep (16mm at top, 18mm
                      at bottom due to wedge taper)

================================================================================
  MOUNTING & ASSEMBLY DETAILS
================================================================================

  Display Mounting:
  - Display module sits in a recessed pocket in front shell
  - Pocket depth: 2.5mm, sized 52.0 x 36.4mm (0.1mm clearance)
  - Held in place by foam tape on back + PCB standoffs pressing against it
  - FPC ribbon folds down and connects to PCB below

  PCB Mounting:
  - 4x M2 screw bosses molded into back shell
  - PCB has 4 mounting holes at corners
  - Standoffs: 3.0mm tall (sets PCB-to-display gap)
  - Screws: M2 x 5mm pan head (from back, through shell into PCB)

  Battery Mounting:
  - Double-sided foam tape to back shell interior
  - Wires routed to PCB connector, strain-relieved with adhesive

  Button Mounting:
  - Tactile switches soldered to PCB
  - Button caps press-fit into front shell holes
  - Caps have internal posts that align with switch actuators
  - Beveled caps: 12 x 6mm top surface, 1mm bevel on all edges
  - Travel: ~0.5mm

  Wake Button:
  - Side-mounted tactile switch on PCB edge
  - Small rocker cap (10 x 4.5mm) press-fit into side cutout
  - Recessed 0.5mm below surface to prevent accidental activation

  Belt Clip:
  - Spring steel clip (0.5mm thick, 20mm wide)
  - Riveted or press-fit into molded pocket on back shell
  - Clip extends ~30mm from body, springs back to ~5mm gap
  - Optional: belt clip is removable (2x micro screws)

  Shell Assembly:
  - Front and back shell snap-fit with 4 internal clips
  - Additional 2x screws at bottom for security (hidden by rubber feet)
  - Gasket: thin foam strip around perimeter for rattle-free fit

================================================================================
  SPECIAL FEATURES
================================================================================

  1. Status LED: RGB LED behind 2mm light-pipe hole, top-left of screen
     - Visible even when device is clipped to belt
     - Pulses gently for notifications

  2. Belt Clip: Integrated spring-steel clip for belt/pocket mounting
     - Rotates slightly for angle adjustment
     - Strong enough for daily carry

  3. Wedge Taper: Bottom is 2mm thicker than top
     - Natural forward-tilt when held or on desk
     - Better screen viewing angle
     - More room at bottom for USB-C connector

  4. Curved Back: 2mm convex dome
     - Comfortable against hip when belt-clipped
     - Prevents rocking on flat surfaces (sits on corner edges)

  5. Vibration Motor: 6mm coin motor on PCB
     - Notification haptics (approval requests, status changes)
     - Pager-authentic buzz feel

  6. Pager Aesthetic:
     - Matte black or dark gray shell
     - Subtle horizontal ridges on sides (grip texture)
     - Small "DEVOTCHI" text debossed on front, below buttons
     - Period-appropriate design language (rounded rectangle, no curves)
