DESCRIPTION
───────────
Turntable-aesthetic desktop companion with a square footprint. The landscape
screen sits in the "album art" display position, angled up ~20 degrees on a
raised section. Surrounding the screen, a circular platter ring decoration
features grooved concentric circles evoking a vinyl record. A decorative
tonearm curves from the right side toward the screen. Three buttons styled
as deck transport controls (play/stop/cue) sit on the front edge. Speaker
fires downward through bottom vents. Dust cover hinge points are decorative
nods to real turntables. Rubber feet keep it stable and allow speaker sound
to escape from below.

MOUNTING DETAILS
────────────────

  Screen Mount:
    - Landscape orientation in platter opening
    - Active area (108×64.8mm) visible through bezel
    - 20° wedge support underneath module
    - 4 clip tabs hold module edges
    - Wedge is integral to top shell interior

  PCB Mount:
    - Horizontal, centered in lower cavity
    - 4 × M2 standoffs (4mm height) into bottom shell
    - Component side up (ESP32 faces screen)
    - Speaker soldered to PCB, faces down toward vents

  Transport Buttons:
    - 3 × 6mm tactile switches on PCB front edge
    - Custom keycaps shaped as transport symbols
    - ▶ (play): 8×8mm cap, triangular profile
    - ■ (stop): 8×8mm cap, square profile
    - ◁◁ (cue): 8×8mm cap, double-triangle profile
    - Through front panel cutouts with 0.3mm clearance

  Speaker:
    - 28mm dia × 3.5mm, mounted face-down on PCB
    - Bottom shell vent: radial slot pattern (12 slots)
    - Rubber feet provide 5mm clearance for sound exit
    - Vent pattern styled as secondary "record grooves"

  USB-C:
    - Rear center of bottom shell
    - 8.94 × 3.26 mm cutout
    - 8mm from bottom edge
    - Accessible while unit sits on desk

  Tonearm:
    - Snap-fit onto pivot post on top shell
    - Single piece: arm + headshell + counterweight
    - Freely rotates on post (or fixed — design choice)
    - Does not contact screen or interfere with display


ASSEMBLY NOTES
──────────────

  1. Press rubber feet into bottom shell (4 corners)
  2. Mount PCB onto bottom shell standoffs (4 × M2 screws)
  3. Verify speaker alignment with bottom vent pattern
  4. Route USB-C to rear cutout, secure with hot-melt
  5. Place screen module on 20° wedge, connect FPC to PCB
  6. Clip screen into bezel tabs
  7. Align top shell over bottom shell
  8. Snap-fit top to bottom (6 snap clips around perimeter)
  9. 2 × M2 security screws on bottom for retention
  10. Snap tonearm onto pivot post
  11. Optionally attach dust cover to hinge points

  Shell: 2-part (top + bottom), snap-fit + 2 screws
  Material: PLA/PETG — matte black for "vinyl" aesthetic
  Print orientation: Top shell face-down, bottom shell face-down
  Accent: Platter ring can be printed in dark gray or painted silver


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

  Platter Ring Grooves:
    - Concentric circular grooves machined/printed into top shell
    - 0.5mm deep, 0.8mm spacing — mimics vinyl record surface
    - Surrounds the screen bezel on all sides
    - Radius: 55mm outer, tapering inward to screen edge
    - Can be enhanced with gloss clear coat for realism

  Tonearm:
    - Curved decorative piece with classic S-shape profile
    - Headshell end points toward screen edge
    - Counterweight ball on opposite end
    - Optional: freely pivots on post for tactile fidget

  Transport Button Caps:
    - Custom-shaped keycaps matching audio player symbols
    - Slightly raised above front panel (1.5mm proud)
    - Contrasting color (silver/chrome look) against black body
    - Satisfying tactile click from underlying 6mm switches

  Downward-Firing Speaker:
    - Speaker faces down through styled vent pattern
    - Rubber feet create acoustic chamber gap
    - Vent slots styled as mini record grooves (matching theme)
    - Desk surface acts as sound reflector

  Dust Cover Hinge Points:
    - Two small posts on rear edge of top shell
    - Accept optional clear/tinted PETG dust cover
    - Cover lifts up and back like real turntable
    - Purely decorative — screen is always accessible

  Living Room Aesthetic:
    - Minimal branding, clean lines
    - Square footprint looks intentional on any desk
    - Matte black finish evokes real audio equipment
    - All cables exit rear — clean front presentation
