================================================================================
  DEVOTCHI PORTABLE CASE — CONCEPT 18: CASSETTE TAPE
================================================================================

  Authentic compact cassette dimensions (100x64x12mm), landscape orientation.
  Screen replaces the transparent tape window area in the center. 3 buttons
  in the label area above the window. Decorative spool circles flank the
  screen. 5 screw hole positions on front face for authenticity. Tape head
  opening at bottom edge = USB-C location. Write-protect tab on top-left
  corner = wake switch. Can physically fit in a cassette deck for fun.
  Ultra-thin 12mm requires slim battery.

  Overall Dimensions: 100mm wide x 64mm tall x 12mm deep
  Active Screen:      40.80 x 30.60mm (landscape, centered in tape window)
  Display Module:     51.80 x 36.20mm
  Weight target:      ~55g with battery
  Orientation:        Landscape
  Shell:              2 pieces (front + back), injection-molded ABS
  Battery:            Custom slim LiPo 60x30x3.5mm (~450mAh)

  NOTE: The cassette form factor provides generous internal space (100x64mm)
  for the 51.80x36.20mm display module. The 12mm depth is tight but feasible
  with a slim battery. The standard 5mm LiPo does NOT fit — requires 3.5mm.

================================================================================
  MOUNTING & ASSEMBLY DETAILS
================================================================================

  Display Mounting:
  - Display module sits in recessed pocket in front shell interior
  - Aligned with tape window opening
  - Held by foam tape and shell clamping pressure
  - Clear lens flush with front surface in window area

  PCB Mounting:
  - 4x M2 standoffs molded into back shell (2mm tall)
  - PCB screws down to standoffs
  - USB-C connector aligns with tape head slot opening
  - Button tactile switches align with front shell button holes

  Battery Mounting:
  - Double-sided foam tape to back shell floor
  - JST-SH 2-pin connector to PCB
  - Positioned offset from center to avoid display FPC

  Shell Assembly:
  - Front and back shells snap together with 6 perimeter snap clips
  - 5x M2 decorative screws on front (2 structural, 3 decorative)
  - Screws go through front shell into back shell standoffs
  - Authentic Phillips head screws match real cassette aesthetic

  Wake Switch:
  - Slide switch PCB-mounted, actuator extends through top shell slot
  - Authentic write-protect tab position (top-left corner)
  - Sliding action: 3mm travel
  - Detent positions for ON/OFF

  1. Authentic Cassette Dimensions

     - Generous internal volume for components

  2. Skeuomorphic Design Language
     - Spool windows with decorative circles flanking screen
     - Label area with printed text above screen

     - Write-protect tab = wake switch
     - 5 authentic screw positions on front

  3. Screen-in-Window Design
     - Display visible through the tape window area
     - Clear parallel to real cassette transparent window
     - Natural visual focus point
     - Spool circles frame the screen aesthetically

  4. Comfortable Pocket Form Factor
     - Flat, rectangular — sits well in shirt/pants pocket
     - 12mm thin — comparable to a phone in a thin case
     - Smooth edges, no protrusions (buttons flush or recessed)
     - Light at ~55g

  5. Customizable Label Area
     - Front label area above screen can be custom printed
     - Users can design their own cassette labels
     - Interchangeable front shell covers possible
     - Back label for regulatory info and serial number

  6. Fidget-Friendly Spools
     - Optional: free-spinning spool decorations
     - Tactile engagement while waiting for approvals

     - Or fixed for reliability

  7. Cultural Reference
     - Cassette tapes are universally recognized
     - Perfect conversation starter for a dev tool
     - Mixtape metaphor: "your AI agent's greatest hits"
     - Works with cassette-themed desk accessories
