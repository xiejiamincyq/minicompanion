DESIGN 20: CASSETTE DECK
========================
Desktop hi-fi rack component form factor. Low-profile landscape slab.
Screen in VU meter position. Faux cassette door hides USB-C port.
Transport-style buttons with LED indicators. Brushed aluminum aesthetic.
Think "1980s stereo component" or "retro hi-fi deck for your desk."

Overall: 170 x 100 x 40mm (W x D x H), landscape, low-profile
Screen: 5.0" SSD1963 touch, 120x75mm module, 108x64.8mm active area
Target user: retro audio enthusiast, desk hi-fi stack aesthetic

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, top to bottom)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — TOP PANEL
    ├── 2mm thick, flat
    ├── 2 rows of ventilation slots (decorative + cooling)
    ├── Brushed/matte finish
    └── Screws into body from above (4x, countersunk) or snap-fit

    Layer 2 — MAIN BODY (faceplate + sides + back as one piece)
    ├── FACEPLATE (front face):
    │   ├── Screen window: 109 x 66mm, upper center
    │   ├── Cassette door opening: ~72 x 22mm, lower left
    │   │   └── Door hinge pocket at bottom of opening
    │   ├── 3x button wells: 10mm dia x 2mm deep, lower right
    │   ├── 3x button LED holes: 2mm dia, below each well
    │   ├── 3x status LED holes: 2mm dia, right of screen
    │   ├── Speaker grille: 28mm dia pattern, left of cassette door
    │   └── Brushed aluminum look (painted or anodized sheet overlay)
    ├── SIDE WALLS: 2mm, plain
    ├── BACK WALL:
    │   ├── USB-C cutout: 8.94 x 3.26mm, left side, centered vertically
    │   └── Plain, brushed finish
    └── BOTTOM: open (closed by bottom panel)

    Layer 3 — SCREEN MODULE (120 x 75 x 4.5mm)
    ├── Landscape orientation
    ├── Pocket in faceplate inner face, 5mm deep
    ├── 4x retention clips
    └── FPC cable exits bottom, routes to PCB below

    Layer 4 — PCB (110 x 65 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 faces down
    ├── SSD1963 40-pin FPC connector (top edge, toward screen)
    ├── 3x tactile switches on front edge (align with button wells)
    ├── USB-C connector on back edge (aligns with rear cutout)
    ├── Speaker connector (JST 2-pin)
    ├── 6x LED positions (3 button + 3 status, front edge)
    └── 4x M2 standoff mounting

    Layer 5 — SPEAKER (28mm dia x 3.5mm)
    ├── Mounted vertically against left inner wall
    ├── Fires through faceplate grille
    ├── Foam gasket for seal
    └── Wire runs to PCB JST connector

    Layer 6 — CASSETTE DOOR (sub-assembly)
    ├── 70 x 20mm panel, 2mm thick
    ├── Piano hinge at bottom edge
    ├── Tinted transparent window (decorative)
    ├── Finger notch at top edge
    └── Spring clip or friction detent

    Layer 7 — BOTTOM PANEL
    ├── 2mm thick, flat
    ├── 4x rubber feet (10mm dia, 2mm tall) at corners
    ├── 4x M2 screw access holes (aligned with body standoffs)
    └── Optional: product label recess


MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────

    Assembly order:
    1. Install screen in faceplate pocket (clips retain)
    2. Connect FPC to PCB
    3. Mount PCB on standoffs inside body (4x M2)
    4. Install speaker against left wall with foam gasket
    5. Connect speaker wire to PCB
    6. Install LED light pipes in faceplate holes
    7. Install cassette door on piano hinge
    8. Attach top panel (snap-fit or 4x countersunk screws)
    9. Attach bottom panel (4x M2 screws from below)
    10. Apply rubber feet to bottom panel corners

    No wall mount — this is a desk-only design.
    Designed to stack with other desk items (flat top + rubber feet).
    USB-C cable exits rear, routes along desk to power.


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

    1. CASSETTE DOOR PANEL
       - Iconic cassette deck aesthetic element
       - Functional: hides USB-C and reset behind a door
       - Tinted window adds retro charm
       - Hinges down smoothly, friction-held positions
       - Can display "NOW PLAYING" label behind window

    2. TRANSPORT BUTTON STYLE
       - ⏮ ⏸ ⏭ icons on button caps (approve/reject/action)
       - Recessed wells prevent accidental presses
       - Satisfying tactile feel in deep wells
       - LED indicators below each button for status feedback
       - Familiar layout for anyone who used a tape deck

    3. BRUSHED ALUMINUM AESTHETIC
       - Faceplate: brushed aluminum look (real sheet or painted)
       - Dark anodized or matte black body
       - Silver button caps with engraved transport symbols
       - Period-correct LED colors (green, red, amber)

    4. RACK-MOUNT STACKABLE FORM
       - Flat top allows stacking other items on top
       - Low profile (40mm) doesn't dominate desk space
       - 170mm width matches mini hi-fi components
       - Rubber feet grip desk surface
       - Could sit in an actual mini equipment rack

    5. VENTILATION DESIGN
       - Top panel vent slots for passive cooling
       - Decorative pattern echoes classic equipment ventilation
       - Adequate airflow for ESP32 thermal management
       - Slots too narrow for debris entry (<2mm)

    6. STATUS LED ARRAY
       - 3 status LEDs right of screen:
         ○ PWR  — power on (green steady)
         ○ CONN — broker connected (green) / disconnected (red)
         ○ ACT  — activity/message flash (amber blink)
       - 3 button LEDs below transport controls:
         ○ Approval pending glow / confirmed flash
       - Adds to "equipment" feel with blinking indicators
