DESCRIPTION
───────────
Car instrument cluster / tachometer style enclosure. Circular body with the
screen visible through a round bezel opening — the "gauge face." A chrome-
look trim ring surrounds the bezel. Three small buttons at the bottom
(7, 5, and 4 o'clock positions) mimic gauge zero/reset buttons. The unit
tilts on a ball-and-socket swivel base for adjustable viewing angle.
Numerical markings (0-100 or RPM style) are printed/silk-screened around the
bezel outside the screen area. Deep dish shape gives satisfying depth. USB-C
on back center. Speaker optionally housed in the swivel base.

OVERALL DIMENSIONS
──────────────────
  Body:  90 mm outer diameter × 45 mm deep (circular)
  Base:  50 mm diameter × 15 mm tall (swivel plate)
  Wall:  2.0 mm
  Total: ~90 mm wide × ~60 mm tall (on base) × 45 mm deep

MOUNTING DETAILS
────────────────

  Screen Mount:
    - Landscape orientation, centered in circular body
    - Rectangular module (120×75mm) fits inside 90mm dia shell
    - Module corners extend beyond visible circle
    - 4 foam pads cushion module against front bezel lip
    - Visible through ~65mm diameter round opening
    - Deep dish hides rectangular module edges completely

  PCB Mount:
    - Behind screen module, separated by 5mm spacer
    - 4 × M2 standoffs into back cap
    - Component side faces back
    - Buttons positioned at lower arc for external access
    - USB-C routes straight to back cap center cutout

  Buttons:
    - 3 × 6mm tactile switches at 7, 5, and 4 o'clock
    - Through-hole in front bezel shell at those positions
    - Small cylindrical button caps (4mm dia, 3mm proud)
    - Styled as gauge reset/zero buttons
    - Chrome or black metal-look caps

  Chrome Trim Ring:
    - Press-fit onto front bezel outer lip
    - 90mm OD, 70mm ID, 3mm cross-section
    - Options: chrome-painted PETG, polished stainless steel
              ring, or chrome vinyl wrap on printed ring
    - Adds premium instrument look

  Gauge Markings:
    - Printed/engraved around bezel between trim ring and screen
    - Numerals: 0-100 in increments of 10 (or 0-8 RPM × 1000)
    - Tick marks: major every 10, minor every 5
    - White or red markings on black bezel
    - Font: DIN-style automotive gauge font
    - "Redline" zone possible at high end

  Ball-and-Socket Swivel:
    - Ball: 15mm diameter, printed integral with back cap
    - Socket: 50mm base plate with hemispherical cavity
    - Friction: tight enough to hold position, loose enough to adjust
    - Tilt range: ±30° from vertical in any direction
    - Rotation: full 360° (cable management needed)
    - Material: PETG-on-PETG for good friction characteristics

  USB-C:
    - Back cap center, 8.94 × 3.26 mm cutout
    - Cable exits straight back
    - Must allow for swivel range without cable strain
    - Recommend right-angle USB-C cable for cleaner routing

  Speaker (optional, in base):
    - 28mm dia × 3.5mm speaker in base plate cavity
    - Sound exits through bottom or side vents in base
    - Wire runs through ball joint center hole
    - If omitted: base is solid weighted disc

  Base Plate:
    - 50mm diameter provides stable footprint
    - Steel weight insert (~50g) for stability
    - Rubber pad on bottom prevents scratching/sliding
    - Low profile (15mm) keeps gauge body prominent


ASSEMBLY NOTES
──────────────

  1. If using speaker: install in base plate, route wire up
  2. Press steel weight into base plate cavity
  3. Apply rubber pad to base bottom
  4. Mount PCB onto back cap standoffs (4 × M2 screws)
  5. Route USB-C to back cap center cutout
  6. Connect button wires/flex to PCB
  7. Connect screen FPC to PCB
  8. Seat screen module against front bezel with foam pads
  9. Insert button caps through front bezel holes
  10. Join back cap to front bezel (snap-fit + 2 × M2 screws)
  11. Press chrome trim ring onto front bezel lip
  12. Snap ball (on back cap) into socket (on base plate)
  13. If speaker present: connect wire through ball joint center

  Shell: 2-part circular (front bezel dish + back cap)
  Base: 1-part with socket cavity
  Material: PLA/PETG — matte black body, chrome accents
  Print orientation: Front bezel face-down, back cap face-down
  Post-processing: Chrome paint or vinyl on trim ring


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

  Round Bezel / Gauge Face:
    - Rectangular screen visible through circular opening
    - Deep dish shape completely hides module corners
    - Software can render circular gauge UI to match
    - The "gauge face" metaphor works perfectly for status display
    - RPM = code velocity, temperature = system health, etc.

  Chrome Trim Ring:
    - Premium instrument appearance
    - Multiple finish options: chrome, brushed aluminum,
      copper, gold, matte gunmetal
    - Removable — can swap rings for different looks
    - Real metal ring option for premium feel

  Gauge Markings:
    - Printed numerals and tick marks around bezel
    - Customizable: RPM, percentage, temperature scale
    - Options for printing:
      a) Laser-engraved into painted surface
      b) Water-slide decal
      c) UV-printed directly
      d) Modeled as raised/recessed geometry in 3D print
    - Red zone marking at high end (optional)

  Ball-and-Socket Swivel:
    - Infinite adjustment angle — tilt to face user
    - Compact mechanism, no external hardware
    - 3D-printable as integral part of shells
    - Can be adjusted one-handed
    - Detachable: gauge lifts off base for transport

  Deep Dish Shape:
    - 45mm total depth creates substantial, weighty feel
    - Hides all electronics behind screen
    - PCB fits comfortably behind screen module
    - Adequate space for wire routing
    - Satisfying visual depth when viewed from angle

  Dashboard Aesthetic:
    - Immediately recognizable instrument cluster look
    - Appeals to car enthusiasts, engineers, makers
    - Pairs well with other desk instruments/gauges
    - Could display real metrics in gauge format:
      CPU load, network speed, build progress
    - Software UI can match physical gauge markings

  Automotive Button Style:
    - Small, precise push buttons at clock positions
    - Mimics trip meter reset / gauge adjustment buttons
    - Chrome or knurled metal-look caps
    - Tactile, purposeful feel
