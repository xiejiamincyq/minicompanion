DESIGN 22: WRIST GAUNTLET
=========================
Cyberpunk wrist-mounted device. Main pod sits on top of the
forearm, screen visible when arm is extended. Angular, aggressive
geometric aesthetic with chamfered edges. Wide 30mm velcro/silicone
strap wraps around forearm with adjustable closure. LED status
strip along one edge. Matte black with accent color lines.

Overall: 70mm wide x 45mm long x 15mm deep (main pod)
Strap: 30mm wide, ~250mm circumference (adjustable)
Weight target: ~50g with battery (pod only), ~65g with strap
Orientation: Landscape (screen on top face)
Shell: 2-piece (top + bottom), 4x hex screws on corners
Aesthetic: Matte black, angular chamfers, accent color edges

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece angular pod (top + bottom), 4x M2 hex screws
  on corners — exposed screw heads are a design feature
- All edges chamfered at 30deg for aggressive angular aesthetic
- Buttons are side-mounted on the outer (right) edge of the pod
  for access with the opposite hand while worn on left wrist
- USB-C on the hand-facing end — cable routes along inner wrist
- LED slot along top edge uses light pipe/diffuser for status glow
- Strap lugs are integral to bottom shell, reinforced to 4mm
- Strap: 30mm wide silicone/velcro, passes through lug slots
- Pod sits ~3mm above the strap surface for airflow
- Vibration motor recessed into bottom shell, contacts forearm
  through strap for direct haptic feedback
- Screen oriented for viewing when arm is extended forward
- Matte black finish with accent color: painted chamfer edges,
  LED glow, and optional colored hex screws (anodized aluminum)

SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Wrist-mounted always-visible display — glanceable status
- Cyberpunk angular aesthetic with aggressive chamfers
- Exposed hex screw industrial design language
- LED status strip along top edge (visible in peripheral vision)
- Vibration haptics felt directly through forearm strap
- Side-mounted buttons accessible with opposite hand
- Strap system allows quick don/doff
- Matte black with accent color theming
- Adjustable strap fits 150-220mm wrist circumference
- Pod can detach from strap for desktop use
