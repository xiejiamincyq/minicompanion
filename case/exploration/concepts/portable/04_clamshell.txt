DESIGN 4: CLAMSHELL / FLIP
==========================
Folds in half to protect screen when not in use. Upper half houses
the screen, lower half has buttons and decoration. Piano hinge along
the middle with ribbon cable pass-through. Magnetic latch keeps it
shut. Think "flip phone" or "Nintendo DS Lite" form factor.

Overall open:   120mm tall x 60mm wide x 10mm deep (each half)
Overall closed: 65mm tall x 60mm wide x 20mm deep
Weight target: ~55g with battery
Orientation: Portrait (open), screen in upper half
Shell: 4 pieces (upper front, upper back, lower front, lower back)

HINGE MECHANISM DETAIL
──────────────────────

    Top view of hinge (looking down at hinge axis):

    ┌── Upper half ──┐   ┌── Lower half ──┐
    │                │   │                │
    │            ╭───┤   ├───╮            │
    │            │ ○─┼───┼─○ │            │ ← 2mm steel pin
    │            ╰───┤   ├───╯            │   through knuckles
    │                │   │                │
    └────────────────┘   └────────────────┘

    Knuckle detail (side view):

    ══╤══╤══╤══╤══╤══╤══╤══╤══  ← Alternating knuckles
      U  L  U  L  U  L  U  L     U = upper half, L = lower half
      │                     │
      ├─ 60mm hinge width ──┤

    - 8 knuckles total (4 per half), 7mm wide each
    - 2mm stainless steel pin, press-fit at ends
    - Center gap between knuckle pair 3 and 4: 8mm wide
      for FPC ribbon cable pass-through

    FPC routing through hinge:

    Upper half    Hinge gap    Lower half
    ┌──────────┐  ┌──────┐  ┌──────────┐
    │  Display │  │      │  │  PCB     │
    │  FPC ────┼──┤ FPC  ├──┼── FPC    │
    │          │  │ loop │  │  conn    │
    └──────────┘  └──────┘  └──────────┘

    FPC makes a U-bend in the hinge gap.
    Service loop allows ~180 degree fold without stress.
    Minimum bend radius: 1.5mm (typical for 0.3mm pitch FPC).

    2x neodymium disc magnets (6mm dia x 2mm thick) in upper half
    2x steel discs (6mm dia x 1mm thick) in lower half

    Magnets press-fit into pockets, secured with adhesive

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- 4-piece shell: upper front + upper back + lower front + lower back
- Upper halves: 2x M2 screws (small, minimal)
- Lower halves: 2x M2 screws
- Piano hinge: 2mm stainless steel pin through 8 knuckles
- Hinge has dedicated 8mm gap for FPC ribbon cable pass-through
- FPC makes U-bend service loop in hinge — accommodates 180deg fold
- Magnetic latch: 2x 6mm neodymium magnets + 2x steel discs
- Screen fully protected when closed — no exposed glass
- PCB and battery both in lower half to keep weight low (stable when open)
- Display in upper half connected via FPC through hinge
- USB-C accessible at bottom edge regardless of open/closed state
- Hinge rated for >10,000 cycles
- Closed dimensions: 65 x 60 x 20mm — pocket-friendly
- Open angle: 0 (closed) to 180 (flat), friction holds at any angle
- Vibration motor in lower half, felt through both halves when closed
