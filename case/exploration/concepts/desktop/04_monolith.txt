DESIGN 4: MONOLITH SLAB
=======================
Minimal portrait slab with integrated kickstand. Think "phone on
a stand" or "2001 monolith" aesthetic. Screen dominates the front face.
Thinnest possible profile.

Overall: 84 x 135 x 15mm slab (portrait)
Target user: minimal desk display, developer status monitor

COMPONENT LAYOUT
────────────────

    FRONT SHELL
    ├── Screen window (41.8 x 31.6mm) — upper 2/3 of face
    ├── LED hole (2mm) below screen
    ├── Screen pocket (52.2 x 36.6mm, 5mm deep from inner face)
    ├── 4x retention clips
    └── Wall: 2mm

    PCB (mounted to back shell)
    ├── 4x standoffs on back shell inner face
    ├── Buttons on RIGHT edge → holes in right side wall
    ├── USB-C on BOTTOM edge → cutout in bottom wall
    ├── ESP32 faces toward back (antenna clearance)
    └── FPC from screen routes directly over top of PCB

    BACK SHELL
    ├── 4x PCB standoff bosses (5mm OD, M2)
    ├── Kickstand slot (52 x 122mm recess, 2.4mm deep)
    ├── Kickstand hinge at top (living hinge or pin hinge)
    ├── USB-C cutout on bottom edge (9.5 x 3.5mm)
    └── Wall: 2mm


ASSEMBLY
────────
- 2-piece clamshell (front + back), 4x M2 screws
- Kickstand is separate piece, pin-hinged to back shell
- Screen drops into pocket from inside before closing
- PCB screws to back shell standoffs
- Extremely simple assembly: 4 screws total + kickstand pin
