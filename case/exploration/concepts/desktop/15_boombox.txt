ASSEMBLY NOTES
══════════════

    1. Body is 2-piece clamshell (front/top shell + back/bottom shell)
       - Front shell: screen opening, speaker grilles, button slots,
         handle mounting points, EQ strip slot
       - Back shell: flat base, battery decoration, USB-C cutout,
         PCB standoffs

    2. Mount PCB to back shell with M2.5 standoffs

    3. Install screen module on internal L-bracket,
       position behind front panel opening

    4. Connect FPC cable from screen to PCB

    5. Mount speaker behind left grille area on bracket
       (wire to PCB speaker pads)

    6. Install button rocker mechanisms in top surface slots
       (align with PCB tactile switches)

    7. Install EQ strip (LED strip or printed decorative insert)
       in front panel slot above screen

    8. Attach handle to top (glue, screw, or snap-fit)

    9. Join front and back shells (screws hidden on bottom)

    10. Apply rubber feet to bottom (4x corners)

    Shell join:
    ┌────────────────────────────┐
    │     Front/top shell        │
    ├────────────────────────────┤ ← Shells meet at mid-height
    │     Back/bottom shell      │   seam line (horizontal split)
    └────────────────────────────┘
    Screws from bottom, hidden by rubber feet


SPECIAL FEATURES
════════════════

    1. CARRY HANDLE
       - Functional handle on top — pick up and carry
       - 15mm thick grip bar, textured surface
       - Fixed or folding (pivot hinge) options
       - Adds personality and portability
       - Boombox identity: carry your Devotchi around

    2. DUAL SPEAKER GRILLES
       - Left grille: functional (28mm speaker behind it)
       - Right grille: decorative (matching pattern, no speaker)
       - Classic boombox symmetry preserved
       - Circular grille pattern with concentric rings

    3. SLIDER ROCKER BUTTONS
       - 3 rocker-style buttons on top surface
       - Rock forward/back action (not push-down)
       - Boombox transport control styling:
         PLAY, FF, STOP (decorative labels)
       - Satisfying clicky rocker action

    4. FAUX EQ DISPLAY
       - Strip above screen on front face
       - Option A: Printed/static decorative bar
       - Option B: Small LED strip showing equalizer animation
         (driven by ESP32 GPIO, reacts to mood/status)
       - Classic boombox visual element

    5. BOOMBOX PROPORTIONS
       - Chunky, wide, aggressive stance
       - 180mm wide — dominant desk presence
       - Boxy body with rounded vertical edges
       - Thick bezels around screen and grilles
       - Unapologetically 1980s aesthetic

    6. BATTERY COMPARTMENT (decorative)
       - Molded battery door on back panel
       - Does not open — purely decorative detail
       - Adds to the "this takes 8 D-cells" illusion
       - Screw heads, latch detail, embossed label

    7. AESTHETIC DETAILS
       - Silver/chrome trim around grilles and screen
       - "DEVOTCHI" badge above screen in chrome letters
       - Faux antenna stub on one corner (optional)
       - Textured front panel (fine crosshatch)
       - Rubberized feet prevent desk scratching
