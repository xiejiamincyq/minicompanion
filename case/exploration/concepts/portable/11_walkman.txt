DESIGN 11: WALKMAN
==================
80s Sony Walkman cassette player homage. Landscape orientation.
Faux cassette window area frames the screen — like peering through the
tape window of a classic portable cassette player. Transport-style
buttons below. Side slider wake control. Belt clip on back.
Color: silver/gray body with orange accents (buttons, trim, clip).

Overall: 100mm wide x 65mm tall x 18mm deep
Weight target: ~55g with battery
Orientation: Landscape
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), 4x M2 screws in corners + snap seam

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, front to back)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT SHELL (100 x 65mm, 1.5mm wall, silver/gray)
    ├── Cassette door frame (raised 0.5mm border, 80 x 45mm)
    ├── Tape window cutout for screen (42.8 x 32.6mm, with 1mm bezel)
    ├── Faux reel hub circles (printed/embossed, decorative only)
    ├── 3x transport-button slots (12.5 x 6.5mm, rectangular)
    ├── 1x headphone jack hole — top edge (3.5mm dia, plugged inside)
    ├── 1x LED hole (2mm dia, lower-left of button area)
    ├── 1x lanyard hole (3mm, top-right corner)
    ├── 4x screen retention clips (L-shaped, 0.5mm overhang)
    ├── 4x screw bosses (M2, in corners)
    └── Decorative EQ bar graphic (printed, lower-right)

    Layer 2 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted landscape (native orientation)
    ├── Centered in cassette window cutout
    ├── FPC cable exits right edge toward PCB connector
    └── Held by front shell retention clips

    Layer 3 — 2.5mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC cable routes from display to PCB connector
    ├── Button plungers pass through (front shell → PCB switches)
    └── Cassette door frame structure provides internal ribbing

    Layer 4 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5mm) faces back
    ├── 3x tactile switches face front (transport button area)
    ├── 1x tactile switch on right edge (wake slider actuates it)
    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge (centered)
    ├── Display FPC connector on right side
    ├── Vibration motor pads near bottom-left
    └── Mounted on 4x posts from back shell

    Layer 5 — 0.5mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module (2.4mm, fits within gap + battery area)

    Layer 6 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo, 300-500mAh)
    ├── Positioned in upper-left quadrant
    ├── JST connector to PCB (short pigtail)
    └── Held by cradle ribs in back shell

    Layer 7 — BACK SHELL (100 x 65mm, 1.5mm wall, silver/gray)
    ├── 4x PCB mounting posts (3mm OD, press-fit)
    ├── Battery cradle ribs (2x, 1mm tall)
    ├── Battery door (snap-off panel, 32 x 37mm)
    ├── Belt clip mounting boss (40mm wide, pivoting hinge)
    ├── Belt clip recess (when folded flat)
    ├── 4x M2 screw holes (corners, countersunk)
    ├── USB-C cutout (9.5 x 4.0mm)
    └── Vibration motor pocket (7mm dia, 3mm deep, foam tape)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece (front + back), 4x M2 countersunk screws in corners
- Additional snap-fit alignment tabs along long edges (2 per side)
- Display press-fits into front shell pocket, held by 4x retention clips
- PCB press-fits onto 4x posts in back shell
- Battery sits in cradle ribs, JST pigtail connects to PCB
- USB-C cutout at bottom center of both shell halves
- Vibration motor adhered inside back shell with foam tape
- Belt clip: spring-steel insert in plastic arm, pivots at top hinge
- Wake slider: rides in channel on right side, spring-return mechanism
- Headphone jack hole: decorative only, plugged behind front shell
- Landscape orientation: display used in native landscape mode
- Orange accents: button caps, slider cap, belt clip arm, trim line
- Surface finish: matte silver/gray with subtle horizontal brushed texture
- Cassette door frame: raised border creates the illusion of a hinged door
- Faux reel hubs: embossed circles flanking the screen inside the frame
