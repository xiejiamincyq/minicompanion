DESIGN 21: TROPHY
=================
Award/achievement display stand. Solid pedestal base with tapered
pillar rising to screen at top. Decorative crown element above screen.
Nameplate recess for engraved label. Gold/metallic finish.
Think "desk award" or "Best AI Agent 2026 trophy."

Overall: 60 x 60mm square base, ~130mm total height
Screen: 5.0" SSD1963 touch, 120x75mm module, 108x64.8mm active area
Target user: celebration display, novelty desk trophy

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, top to bottom)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Part 1 — CROWN ELEMENT (~30 x 30 x 20mm)
    ├── Decorative star, cup, or laurel wreath
    ├── Press-fit or screw onto screen housing top
    ├── Solid (no electronics)
    ├── Gold/metallic painted finish
    └── Interchangeable: different crowns for different moods

    Part 2 — SCREEN HOUSING (~80 x 50 x 16mm)
    ├── Screen window: 109 x 66mm on front face
    ├── Screen pocket: 121 x 76mm, 5mm deep
    ├── 4x retention clips for screen module
    ├── PCB standoffs inside (4x M2)
    ├── Tilted ~10° forward from vertical
    ├── Bottom opening connects to pillar top
    └── 2-piece clamshell (front + back half)

    Part 3 — SCREEN MODULE (120 x 75 x 4.5mm)
    ├── Landscape orientation
    ├── Seated in housing front pocket
    ├── Touch digitizer faces front
    └── FPC exits bottom, routes into pillar

    Part 4 — PCB (110 x 65 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5 x 2.4mm) faces back
    ├── SSD1963 40-pin FPC connector
    ├── No tactile switches on PCB (buttons in base, wired up)
    ├── USB-C connector → wired to breakout in base via pillar
    │   (or: USB-C on PCB, extension cable through pillar)
    ├── Speaker connector header
    └── 4x M2 mounting to housing standoffs

    Part 5 — PILLAR (~30mm OD, ~70mm tall)
    ├── Hollow tube, tapers from 30mm base to 20mm top
    ├── Internal channel ~12mm dia for wiring
    ├── Wires: FPC extension, USB-C extension, speaker, buttons
    ├── Press-fits into base socket + screen housing socket
    ├── Can be printed as separate piece or integral with base
    └── Gold/metallic finish, smooth

    Part 6 — BASE PEDESTAL (60 x 60 x 20mm)
    ├── Hollow interior
    ├── 3x tactile switches on front edge (6mm, through holes)
    ├── Nameplate recess on front face: 42 x 14mm, 1mm deep
    │   └── Accepts engraved metal strip (adhesive-backed)
    ├── USB-C breakout board or extension port on back face
    │   └── Cutout: 8.94 x 3.26mm
    ├── Speaker (28mm dia x 3.5mm) mounted face-down
    │   └── Sound exits through slot in base bottom
    │   └── Desk surface acts as acoustic reflector
    ├── Pillar socket (center top, 30mm dia recess)
    ├── 4x rubber feet on bottom (8mm dia, 2mm tall)
    │   └── Raised feet create gap for speaker sound to exit
    └── Bottom panel: removable (2x screws) for assembly access

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────

    Assembly order:
    1. Install screen in housing front pocket (clips retain)
    2. Mount PCB on housing standoffs (4x M2)
    3. Connect FPC from screen to PCB
    4. Route extension wires through pillar:
       - Speaker wire (2-conductor)
       - Button wires (3x 2-conductor or ribbon)
       - USB-C extension cable
    5. Press-fit pillar into screen housing bottom socket
    6. Press-fit pillar into base top socket
    7. Connect speaker to wire in base
    8. Connect buttons to wires in base
    9. Connect USB-C breakout in base
    10. Close base bottom panel (2x M2 screws)
    11. Apply rubber feet
    12. Press-fit crown element on top
    13. Insert nameplate into recess

    Disassembly: pull crown off, pull pillar from base,
    pull pillar from housing. Three main pieces separate.


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

    1. TROPHY / AWARD AESTHETIC
       - Instantly recognizable desk trophy silhouette
       - Gold/metallic finish elevates perceived quality
       - Nameplate makes it feel like a real award
       - Conversation starter: "What did you win?"
       - Celebrates the AI agent partnership concept

    2. INTERCHANGEABLE CROWN ELEMENTS
       - Press-fit top decoration can be swapped:
         ★ Star — default, classic trophy
         🏆 Cup — achievement unlocked
         ☆ Laurel wreath — winner's crown
         ◇ Diamond — premium feel
       - Print multiples, swap for mood or occasion
       - Could 3D print custom crowns

    3. NAMEPLATE PERSONALIZATION
       - Recess accepts standard engraved plates
       - "Best AI Agent 2026"
       - "Most Helpful Coder"
       - "Ship It Award"
       - User personalizes their trophy

    4. TILTED SCREEN VIEWING ANGLE
       - 10° forward tilt angles screen toward seated user
       - Natural viewing from slightly below (desk height)
       - Screen at eye-friendly angle, no neck strain
       - Touch accessible from front

    5. SPEAKER IN BASE
       - 28mm speaker fires down, reflects off desk surface
       - Rubber feet create acoustic gap (2mm clearance)
       - Base acts as resonance chamber (60x60x20mm cavity)
       - Sound projects outward from base perimeter
       - Achievement unlock sound effects

    6. MODULAR THREE-PIECE DESIGN
       - Crown + screen housing + pillar + base
       - Each piece separates for maintenance
       - Pillar press-fits into sockets (no tools needed)
       - Could print different pillar heights
       - Different bases possible (round, octagonal, etc.)

    7. GOLD FINISH OPTIONS
       - PLA/PETG: gold metallic filament
       - Resin: metallic gold resin or gold spray paint
       - Premium: vacuum-metallized plastic coating
       - Budget: gold spray paint over primer
       - Accent: black base with gold pillar and crown
