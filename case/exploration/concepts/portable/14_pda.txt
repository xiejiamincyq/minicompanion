DESIGN 14: PDA
==============
Palm Pilot / early PocketPC homage. Portrait orientation.
Screen fills the upper 70% of the front face. Below: 4 round
app-launcher buttons in a row with silk-screened icons, tiny
nav rocker in center. Stylus slot on right edge. Infrared
window decoration at top. Graffiti writing area below buttons.
Think "Palm III" meets Tamagotchi pet.

Overall: 75mm tall x 55mm wide x 16mm deep
Weight target: ~52g with battery
Orientation: Portrait
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), 4x screw posts + snap alignment

──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT SHELL (rectangular, rounded corners, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── Screen window cutout (41.8 x 31.6mm, portrait)
    ├── IR window slot at top edge (50mm x 3mm, for translucent insert)
    ├── 4x round button holes (8.5mm dia) in launcher row
    ├── 1x nav rocker opening (10 x 10mm, center)
    ├── Graffiti zone: textured recessed area (45 x 12mm, 0.3mm deep)
    ├── 1x LED hole (2mm dia, above screen)
    ├── Stylus channel on right wall (55mm long, 4mm wide, 2mm deep)
    ├── 4x screen retention clips (L-shaped)
    └── 4x screw post sockets (M2 threaded inserts)

    Layer 2 — IR WINDOW INSERT (dark red translucent plastic)
    ├── 50mm x 3mm x 2mm strip
    ├── Press-fits into slot at top of front shell
    └── Purely decorative (no actual IR hardware)

    Layer 3 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted portrait (long axis vertical)
    ├── Seated in pocket, 0.2mm clearance per side
    ├── FPC cable exits bottom edge
    └── Held by front shell retention clips

    Layer 4 — 2.5mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC cable routes to PCB connector
    └── Button plungers pass through

    Layer 5 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5mm) faces back
    ├── 4x tactile switches for launcher buttons (face front)
    ├── 1x 5-way nav switch (center, face front)
    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge
    ├── Display FPC connector on top edge
    └── Vibration motor pads

    Layer 6 — 0.5mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module height

    Layer 7 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo, 300-500mAh)
    ├── Centered in device
    ├── JST connector pigtail to PCB
    └── Held by cradle ribs in back shell

    Layer 8 — BACK SHELL (rectangular, rounded corners, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── 4x screw posts (M2, 8mm tall)
    ├── Battery cradle ribs (2x)
    ├── USB-C cutout (9.5 x 4.0mm)
    ├── Stylus channel continuation (right wall)
    └── Recessed label area (engraved or sticker)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece rectangular with rounded corners (3mm radius)
- Front + back joined by 4x M2 screws (recessed into back shell)
- Alignment via 2x registration pins on seam edge
- Display press-fits into front shell pocket, 4x retention clips
- PCB screws onto 4x posts in back shell
- Battery in cradle ribs, JST pigtail to PCB
- USB-C cutout at bottom center, aligned across both halves
- Vibration motor foam-taped to inside of back shell
- IR window insert press-fits into top edge slot
- Stylus channel molded into right wall of both shell halves
- Graffiti zone is recessed texture on front shell (no cutout)
- Nav rocker center-click doubles as wake function
- Portrait orientation: screen dominates upper 70% of face


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- IR window: Dark red translucent plastic insert at top edge.
  Purely decorative homage to Palm Pilot IR beaming port.
  Glows faintly when internal LED shines through (status indicator).
- Stylus slot: Channel along right edge holds a mini aluminum
  stylus (50mm). Friction fit with slight taper. Can be real
  capacitive stylus or decorative ribbed insert.
- Graffiti zone: Textured/stippled recessed area below buttons.
  Silk-screened divider line separating "abc" and "123" zones.
  Purely tactile/visual homage — not touch-sensitive.
- App launcher buttons: 4 round buttons with silk-screened icons
  (calendar, mail, menu, home). Evokes Palm's hardware launcher.
- Nav rocker: 5-way navigation pad in center below buttons.
  Center click = wake. Directional = future menu navigation.
- Two-tone plastic: Front shell in dark gray, back in lighter
  gray or metallic silver — classic PDA color scheme.
- Recessed label area on back: "Devotchi PDA Model P-1000"
  engraved or printed — product identity detail.
