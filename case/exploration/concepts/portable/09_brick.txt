================================================================================
  DEVOTCHI PORTABLE CASE - CONCEPT 09: FIDGET BRICK
================================================================================

  Chunky, satisfying-to-hold brick. Extra thick for ergonomic comfort with
  very rounded edges and corners. Feels like a smooth river stone. Textured
  grip zones on sides. Domed back. Larger battery thanks to extra depth.

  Overall Dimensions: 65mm tall x 50mm wide x 22mm deep
  Edge Radius:        8mm on ALL edges and corners (heavily rounded)

================================================================================
  MOUNTING & ASSEMBLY DETAILS
================================================================================

  Display Mounting:
  - Recessed pocket in front shell: 52.0 x 36.4mm
  - Pocket is 0.5mm below front surface (screen sits below surface)
  - Protective lip around screen prevents direct contact with flat surfaces
  - Thin foam gasket between display and shell (vibration isolation)

  PCB Mounting:
  - 4x M2 screw bosses in back shell
  - Brass threaded inserts pressed into bosses (durable)
  - Standoffs: 3.0mm (sets display-to-PCB gap)
  - M2 x 6mm screws from back side

  Battery Mounting:
  - Deep pocket in back shell (8mm + walls)
  - Battery held by friction fit + foam pad on PCB side
  - JST PH 2.0mm connector, short pigtail
  - Kapton tape wrap on battery for insulation

  Button Mounting:
  - Domed caps sit in counterbored holes in front shell
  - Cap posts pass through shell to engage tactile switches
  - Caps retained by small shoulder (snap in, won't fall out)
  - Smooth dome surface for satisfying finger feel

  Wake Button:
  - Top edge, centered
  - Flush cap: 10 x 4.5mm
  - Slightly recessed (0.3mm) to blend with rounded top
  - Natural thumb position when holding device

  Shell Assembly:
  - Front and back shells held by 4x M2 screws (from back)
  - Screws hidden under rubber plugs (clean look)
  - No snap clips (screws only -- solid, no rattles)
  - Thin silicone gasket at shell seam

  Seam Line:
  - Shell splits along the midplane (11mm from front, 11mm from back)
  - Seam runs around perimeter, follows rounded edges
  - Tight tolerance: 0.05mm gap (barely visible)

================================================================================
  SPECIAL FEATURES
================================================================================

  1. Fidget-Friendly Design:
     - Domed buttons invite pressing (satisfying tactile click)
     - Rounded edges are nice to run fingers along
     - Grip texture provides sensory feedback
     - Overall shape is pleasant to hold and fidget with
     - Weight: ~45-50g (satisfying heft)

  2. River Stone Ergonomics:
     - 8mm radius on all edges (no sharp corners anywhere)
     - Back dome fits palm naturally
     - Can be cupped in hand, rolled between palms
     - Comfortable in pocket (won't poke or jab)

  3. Extended Battery Life:
     - Extra depth allows 700-800mAh cell
     - ~60-70% more capacity than standard design
     - Full day+ of active use, multiple days standby

  4. Textured Grip Zones:
     - Raised dot pattern on both sides
     - Prevents slipping when held
     - Subtle enough to not snag in pockets
     - Adds visual interest to the design

  5. Recessed Screen:
     - Screen sits 0.5mm below front surface
     - Protective lip prevents screen contact when face-down
     - Chamfered edge around screen cutout (nice visual detail)

  6. Solid Construction:
     - Screw-only assembly (no snap clips to break)
     - Brass inserts for durable threads
     - Thick walls + rounded geometry = very strong
     - Drop-resistant design (rounded edges distribute impact)

  7. Premium Feel:
     - Heavier than needed (deliberate -- feels substantial)
     - Soft-touch coating option on entire shell
     - Tight tolerances, minimal seam gap
     - Quality tactile switches with audible click
