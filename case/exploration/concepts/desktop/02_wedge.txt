DESIGN 2: WEDGE CONSOLE
=======================
Low-profile landscape wedge. 155 x 100mm footprint, 40mm at back
tapering to 10mm at front. Screen embedded in angled top surface
(~15° tilt). Think "alarm clock" or "retro game console" aesthetic.

Overall: 155 x 100 x 10–40mm (wedge taper)
Target user: desk console, always-on status display

COMPONENT LAYOUT (top to bottom inside wedge)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

    TOP SHELL (wedge shape)
    ├── Angled screen pocket on top surface (~15° tilt)
    │   ├── Screen window cutout (41.8 x 31.6mm)
    │   ├── 4x retention clips around screen edges
    │   └── Pocket depth: module thickness + 0.3mm
    ├── 3x button holes on front lip (5mm dia)
    ├── LED hole on front lip (2mm dia)
    ├── Vent slots on back face (for heat)
    └── Wall: 2mm, uniform

    INTERNALS (stacked front-to-back inside wedge)
    ├── Screen module (51.8 x 36.2mm) in angled pocket
    ├── PCB mounted on 4x standoffs from bottom plate
    │   ├── Standoffs: 5mm OD, M2 screw, 4mm tall
    │   ├── USB-C connector faces back edge
    │   └── Tactile switches face up toward button holes
    └── Speaker (28mm dia) in cavity near back-bottom
        ├── Fires downward through bottom plate grille
        └── 0.5mm foam gasket for acoustic seal

    BOTTOM PLATE
    ├── Speaker grille (grid of 1.5mm holes, 30% open area)
    ├── 4x screw bosses (align with top shell)
    ├── 4x rubber feet (8mm dia, 1.5mm tall)
    └── Wall: 2mm

ASSEMBLY
────────
- 2-piece: top shell + bottom plate
- 4x M2 screws from bottom into top shell bosses
- Screen drops into angled pocket from above before closing
- PCB screws to bottom plate standoffs
- Speaker press-fit into bottom plate cavity with foam gasket
