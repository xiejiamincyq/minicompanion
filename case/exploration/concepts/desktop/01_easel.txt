DESIGN 1: DESKTOP EASEL
=======================
Tilted slab on a triangular stand. Screen faces upward at ~65° angle.
Think "digital picture frame" or "desk clock" form factor.

Overall: 130 x 85 x 14mm slab, ~110mm wide stand, ~70mm tall
Target user: desk/nightstand display

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, front to back)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT SHELL
    ├── Screen window cutout (41.8 x 31.6mm)
    ├── 3x button holes (5mm dia, below screen)
    ├── 1x LED hole (2mm, top-right of screen)
    ├── 4x screen retention clips (L-shaped, 0.5mm overhang)
    └── Wall: 2mm

    Layer 2 — SCREEN MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 4.5mm)
    ├── Seated in pocket with 0.2mm clearance per side
    ├── FPC cable exits bottom edge
    └── Held by retention clips from front shell

    Layer 3 — 3mm AIR GAP
    └── FPC cable routes through here to PCB connector

    Layer 4 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm, estimated)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 faces back
    ├── Tactile switches face front (through button holes)
    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge
    └── Mounted on 4x standoffs (5mm OD, M2 screws)

    Layer 5 — BACK SHELL
    ├── 4x standoff bosses (screw into front shell bosses)
    ├── USB-C cutout (9.5 x 3.5mm) on bottom edge
    └── Wall: 2mm

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Slab: 2-piece clamshell (front + back), 4x M2 screws at corners
- Stand: separate piece, slab drops into groove
- Groove friction-fit (no adhesive), 0.2mm clearance per side
- USB-C accessible from bottom edge of slab, even when in stand
- Rubber feet on stand base (4x, 8mm dia, 1.5mm tall)
