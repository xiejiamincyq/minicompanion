ASSEMBLY NOTES
══════════════

    1. Mill/CNC the wooden base:
       - 110x90x15mm block (walnut, oak, or bamboo)
       - Mill cavity for PCB (112x67mm, 5mm deep, centered)
       - Drill 3x button holes on front edge (6mm dia)
       - Mill speaker recess on bottom (28mm dia, 8mm deep)
       - Drill speaker sound holes through base bottom
       - Drill USB-C cutout on back face (8.94x3.26mm)
       - Drill 4x alignment pin holes for cube (2mm dia)

    2. Mount PCB into base cavity:
       - 4x M2 screws from base bottom into PCB standoffs
       - PCB sits flush or 1mm below base top surface

    3. Install brass button caps:
       - Button caps sit proud of base surface by 2mm
       - Spring-loaded stems press PCB tactile switches
       - Press-fit or threaded into base

    4. Assemble screen bracket:
       - Attach screen module to L-bracket (2x M2)
       - Attach bracket to PCB (2x M2 standoffs)
       - Connect FPC cable

    5. Place alignment pins in base (4x press-fit brass pins)

    6. Add decorative items around screen inside cube area

    7. Lower cube over assembly, aligning with pins

    Cable routing:
    - FPC from screen to PCB: short run, bracket-mounted
    - Speaker wires: PCB to speaker in base recess
    - USB-C: PCB connector aligns with base back cutout


SPECIAL FEATURES
════════════════

    1. TRANSPARENT DISPLAY CUBE
       - Screen visible from front, back, and both sides
       - PCB components become part of the aesthetic
       - Electronics-as-art: circuit board visible through glass
       - 3mm clear acrylic or tempered glass construction

    2. REMOVABLE CUBE
       - Cube lifts straight off base (no fasteners)
       - Alignment pins keep it positioned
       - Easy access to arrange decorative items
       - Swap cubes: tinted, frosted, or custom-etched

    3. TERRARIUM SPACE
       - ~30mm of space around screen on all sides
       - Place small items: mini succulents (faux), figurines,
         crystals, pebbles, moss, tiny scene dioramas
       - Personalize your Devotchi's living space
       - Items visible through all transparent walls

    4. NATURAL MATERIAL BASE
       - Warm wood contrasts with clear cube
       - Options: walnut (dark), oak (light), bamboo (modern)
       - Brass/copper button caps add premium tactile feel
       - Visible wood grain, oil-finished

    5. DOWNWARD-FIRING SPEAKER
       - Speaker in base fires downward through holes
       - Desk surface acts as resonance amplifier
       - Subtle, ambient sound quality
       - Rubber feet create air gap for sound

    6. DISPLAY CABINET AESTHETIC
       - Museum/curiosity cabinet vibes
       - Devotchi creature "lives" in its habitat
       - Conversation starter on any desk
       - Can be lit with small LED strip inside cube
         for ambient glow (optional future addition)
