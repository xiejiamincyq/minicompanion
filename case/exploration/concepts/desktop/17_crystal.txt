DESIGN 17: CRYSTAL
==================
Geometric gem/crystal shape — irregular faceted polyhedron.
Screen mounted on the largest flat facet, angled ~20° backward.
Transparent/frosted resin shell with internal RGB LED ambient glow.
Think "desk geode" or "digital gemstone" aesthetic.

Overall: 80 x 60 x 100mm (W x D x H)
Screen: 5.0" SSD1963 touch, 120x75mm module, 108x64.8mm active area
Target user: designer desk, conversation piece

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, outside to inside)
───────────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — CRYSTAL SHELL (2-piece: upper + base)
    ├── Upper crystal body
    │   ├── Screen window cutout (109 x 66mm) on main facet
    │   ├── Multiple decorative facets (5-7 flat faces)
    │   ├── Material: clear/frosted resin or translucent PETG
    │   ├── Wall thickness: 2mm throughout
    │   └── Internal cavity ~50mm deep for light diffusion
    ├── Base section
    │   ├── 3x button holes (6mm dia) on front edge of base facet
    │   ├── Flat-cut bottom for stability
    │   ├── 4x M2 standoff bosses (rising from base floor)
    │   └── USB-C cutout (8.94 x 3.26mm) on back facet, near base
    └── Join: base screws into upper (4x M2 from bottom)

    Layer 2 — SCREEN MODULE (120 x 75 x 4.5mm)
    ├── Seated in pocket on main facet inner wall
    ├── 4x retention clips (L-shaped, 0.5mm overhang)
    ├── FPC/ribbon cable exits bottom edge
    └── Touch digitizer faces outward through window

    Layer 3 — 3mm AIR GAP / WIRING ZONE
    ├── FPC cable routes to PCB connector
    └── RGB LED wiring

    Layer 4 — PCB (110 x 65 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5 x 2.4mm) faces inward
    ├── 3x tactile switches on front edge (align with base holes)
    ├── USB-C connector on back edge
    ├── 2-4x RGB LEDs (WS2812B or similar) facing into cavity
    ├── Mounted on 4x M2 standoffs (5mm height)
    └── SSD1963 display connector (40-pin FPC)

    Layer 5 — INTERNAL CAVITY (behind PCB)
    └── Hollow space for RGB light to fill and diffuse
        through translucent shell walls

    - 2-piece shell split along a natural facet edge
    - Upper crystal loads screen + PCB from open bottom
    - Base screws up into upper (4x M2 from underneath)
    - Joint line follows a facet edge so it's nearly invisible
    - USB-C cable exits low on back, hidden from front view
    - No speaker (keeps crystal minimal and sealed for light)
    - Optional: tiny piezo buzzer on PCB for beep feedback


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

    1. RGB AMBIENT GLOW
       - 2-4x addressable RGB LEDs on PCB rear face
       - Light fills hollow cavity behind PCB
       - Diffuses through translucent/frosted shell walls
       - Non-screen facets glow with mood color
       - Can pulse, breathe, or shift hue based on agent state
       - Mood mapping: happy=green, sad=blue, angry=red,
         thinking=amber pulse, sleeping=dim purple breathe

    2. FACETED SILHOUETTE
       - Irregular angles catch desk lamp light differently
       - Each facet edge creates a highlight line
       - Crystal shape is recognizable from any viewing angle
       - Frosted finish scatters light beautifully

    3. MATERIAL OPTIONS
       - Clear resin: maximum light transmission, see internals
       - Frosted resin: soft diffused glow, hides internals
       - Tinted resin: amethyst purple, rose quartz pink, etc.
       - Opaque with translucent inlay strips along facet edges

    4. FLAT BASE STABILITY
       - Base facet is deliberately wide (70x55mm)
       - Low center of gravity despite tall form
       - Optional adhesive rubber pad on base bottom
       - Crystal leans slightly back (20°) — naturally stable

    5. TOUCH SCREEN INTERACTION
       - SSD1963 capacitive touch through screen window
       - Buttons are backup/secondary controls only
       - Crystal shape encourages reaching out to touch the face
