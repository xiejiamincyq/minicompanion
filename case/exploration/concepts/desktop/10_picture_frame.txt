DESCRIPTION
───────────
Digital photo frame aesthetic designed to blend into any living room or desk.
Wide decorative bezel with wood grain or bamboo texture pattern surrounds the
screen behind a clear panel. From the front, it looks like a normal small
picture frame — no visible buttons, no obvious tech. Three buttons are
completely hidden on the bottom edge (touch to find, invisible from front).
An easel-style fold-out back stand props it up at a comfortable viewing
angle. USB-C is recessed on the bottom edge. Optional wall-mount keyhole
slot on back. The cleanest, most discreet Devotchi design.

OVERALL DIMENSIONS
──────────────────
  Width:  160 mm
  Height: 120 mm
  Depth:   25 mm  (body) / ~80 mm (with easel deployed)
  Wall:     2.0 mm (structural) + 20 mm decorative bezel
  Bezel:   20 mm on all sides

MOUNTING DETAILS
────────────────

  Screen Mount:
    - Landscape, centered behind front panel opening
    - Perimeter foam tape (2mm thick) cushions module
    - Front clear panel provides protection
    - 1mm bezel lip overlap on screen edges
    - No clips needed — sandwiched between shells

  PCB Mount:
    - Behind screen, separated by 2mm foam spacer
    - 4 × M2 standoffs into back shell (3mm height)
    - Component side faces back (speaker toward back vents)
    - Buttons on daughter board or flex cable to bottom edge

  Hidden Buttons:
    - 3 × 6mm tactile switches
    - Mounted on small flex PCB along bottom edge
    - Actuated through 1mm holes in bottom edge
    - No visible marking — user finds by touch
    - Slight tactile bump (0.2mm) optional for findability
    - 20mm spacing, centered on bottom edge

  Speaker:
    - 28mm dia × 3.5mm on PCB, fires toward back
    - Sound exits through small vent holes in back shell
    - Vent holes in bottom area — invisible from front
    - Modest volume — appropriate for desktop distance

  USB-C:
    - Recessed 3mm into bottom edge
    - 8.94 × 3.26 mm cutout
    - Hidden from front and side views
    - Cable exits downward — invisible on desk

  Wall Mount (optional):
    - Keyhole slot on upper back: 10mm head, 5mm neck
    - Accepts standard picture hook or screw
    - Centered horizontally, 25mm from top edge
    - Load capacity: sufficient for ~150g total weight

  Easel Stand:
    - Piano hinge or living hinge at top of leg
    - Friction detent holds at ~20° from vertical
    - When deployed, creates ~70° viewing angle
    - Folds completely flat (1.5mm profile)
    - Rubber foot on bottom tip prevents sliding


ASSEMBLY NOTES
──────────────

  1. Clean clear front panel, place into front frame recess
  2. Apply foam tape perimeter to screen module
  3. Seat screen module behind clear panel, press into foam
  4. Mount PCB onto back shell standoffs (4 × M2 screws)
  5. Connect button flex cable from PCB to bottom edge
  6. Connect screen FPC to PCB
  7. Route USB-C to bottom edge cutout
  8. Attach easel leg to back shell hinge point
  9. Join front frame to back shell
  10. Secure with 4 × snap clips (hidden in frame edges)
  11. Optional: 2 × M2 screws from back for security

  Shell: 2-part (front frame + back plate)
  Material: PLA/PETG with wood-grain texture filament
           or standard filament + wood grain vinyl wrap
  Clear panel: 1.5mm acrylic or polycarbonate, laser-cut
  Print orientation: Front frame face-down, back plate flat
  Finish: Matte — avoid glossy plastic look


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

  Invisible Technology:
    - From the front: looks like an ordinary picture frame
    - No visible buttons, ports, LEDs, or branding
    - Clear panel could pass for glass
    - Only the screen content reveals it is electronic
    - Perfect for living room, office, or bedside table

  Wood Grain / Bamboo Texture:
    - 20mm wide decorative bezel on all sides
    - Options for achieving texture:
      a) Wood-fill PLA filament (real wood particles)
      b) Standard PLA + printed vinyl wrap
      c) Textured surface in 3D model (grain lines)
      d) Real wood veneer glued to PLA frame
    - Multiple finish options: oak, walnut, bamboo, white

  Hidden Button Interface:
    - Buttons on bottom edge — invisible from all angles
    - User learns positions through muscle memory
    - Optional: tiny tactile bumps (0.2mm) as guides
    - Feels intentional, not like missing buttons
    - Alternative: capacitive touch pads on bottom edge

  Easel Stand:
    - Classic picture frame back stand design
    - Allows portrait or landscape orientation on desk
    - Folds flat for transport or wall mounting
    - Friction-fit holds angle without ratcheting
    - Rubber tip grips desk surface

  Wall Mount Option:
    - Keyhole slot accepts standard picture hanging hardware
    - No easel needed when wall-mounted
    - Looks like a digital picture frame on any wall
    - USB-C cable can be routed downward along wall

  Glass/Clear Panel:
    - Protects screen from dust and fingerprints
    - Creates authentic frame appearance
    - Anti-glare treatment optional
    - Adds perceived quality and weight
    - Replaceable if scratched

  Living Room Friendly:
    - Zero "gadget" aesthetic — pure home decor
    - No fan noise, no visible wiring from front
    - Size comparable to 4×6" photo in frame
    - Could display clock, art, or ambient animations
    - Partners and guests won't object to its presence
