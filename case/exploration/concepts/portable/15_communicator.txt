DESIGN 15: COMMUNICATOR
=======================
Star Trek communicator badge shape. Upward-pointing arrowhead/
chevron/shield form factor. Screen centered in the widest part.
Buttons as small raised dots on the lower chevron point. Pin clip
and magnet mount on back for shirt/jacket attachment. Very thin
profile — custom slim battery. Tap-to-wake via accelerometer.
Gold/silver two-tone finish. Wearable, iconic, conversation piece.

Overall: 65mm tall x 50mm wide x 12mm deep
Weight target: ~30g with battery (wearable-light)
Orientation: Portrait (screen landscape within chevron)
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), ultrasonic weld seam

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, front to back)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT SHELL (chevron shape, 1.0mm wall)
    ├── Screen window cutout (41.8 x 31.6mm)
    ├── 3x button holes (5.5mm dia, in lower chevron)
    ├── 1x LED hole (1.5mm dia, upper chevron)
    ├── Screen retention: 4x clips around window
    ├── Finish: Gold-tone metallic paint or anodized look
    └── Ultrasonic weld ridge around perimeter

    Layer 2 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Centered in widest part of chevron
    ├── Landscape orientation within portrait badge
    ├── FPC exits bottom edge toward PCB
    └── Held by front shell retention clips

    Layer 3 — 1.5mm AIR GAP (reduced from standard 2.5mm)
    ├── FPC routing
    ├── Button plunger extensions
    └── Accelerometer clearance on PCB top side

    Layer 4 — PCB (custom shape to fit chevron, 1.6mm thick)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5mm) faces back
    ├── Accelerometer (LIS2DH12, 2x2mm) for tap-to-wake
    ├── 3x micro tactile switches (face front, lower area)
    ├── USB-C connector at bottom point
    ├── Display FPC connector at top
    ├── Vibration motor pads
    └── PCB outline follows chevron contour (custom shape)

    Layer 5 — 0.4mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module + accelerometer

    Layer 6 — BATTERY (custom slim LiPo, 3.5mm thick)
    ├── ~35 x 25mm footprint (fits chevron mid-section)
    ├── 200-250mAh capacity (slim trade-off)
    ├── JST connector pigtail to PCB
    └── Foam pad cushion in back shell cradle

    Layer 7 — BACK SHELL (chevron shape, 1.0mm wall)
    ├── Slightly convex outer surface (1mm crown)
    ├── Magnet recess (10mm dia, 2mm deep pocket)
    ├── Pin clip mounting bosses (2x screw posts)
    ├── Battery cradle with foam pad
    ├── USB-C cutout at bottom point
    ├── Finish: Silver-tone metallic paint
    └── Ultrasonic weld ridge (mates with front shell)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece chevron (front + back), ultrasonic weld
- No screws — welded seam for thin, clean profile
- Seam runs around chevron perimeter at mid-depth
- Display press-fits into front shell, 4x retention clips
- PCB is custom chevron-contour shape, press-fits on 3x posts
- Battery cushioned with foam pad in back shell cradle
- USB-C cutout at bottom vertex, tight clearance
- Magnet inset into machined pocket in back wall (flush)
- Pin clip attached via 2x M1.4 micro screws into bosses
- Vibration motor foam-taped inside back shell
- Accelerometer on PCB enables tap-to-wake (no physical wake btn)
- Assembly: front shell + internals, then ultrasonic weld back
- Service access: pry tool along weld seam (re-weldable)
- Companion magnet disc included (15mm steel disc with adhesive)


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Chevron/arrowhead shape: Instantly recognizable Star Trek
  communicator badge silhouette. Conversation starter.
- Two-tone finish: Front shell in gold metallic, back shell
  in silver metallic. Premium look. Can be painted ABS or
  metallic-filled resin for 3D printing.
- Tap-to-wake: LIS2DH12 accelerometer on PCB detects double-tap
  gesture. No physical wake button needed — keeps the thin profile
  and clean lines. Tap the badge to wake your Devotchi.
- Pin clip + magnet dual mount: Wear it on a shirt pocket (clip)
  or magnetically attach through fabric (companion disc inside
  shirt). Both mounting methods included.
- Slim 12mm profile: Achieved with thinner 1.0mm walls and
  custom 3.5mm LiPo cell. Wearable as a badge without bulk.
- Raised dot buttons: Three hemispherical 5mm buttons on the
  lower chevron taper. Color-coded dots (red/blue/green).
  Tactile and findable by touch.
- Convex back: 1mm crown on back surface for comfortable wear
  against the body. No sharp edges anywhere.
- LED at top vertex: Status indicator visible from across a room.
  Shines through 1.5mm hole in upper chevron area.
