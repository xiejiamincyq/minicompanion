================================================================================
  DEVOTCHI PORTABLE CASE - CONCEPT 08: LANYARD BADGE
================================================================================

  ID badge form factor designed to hang from a lanyard. Landscape screen in
  upper 2/3, thin profile. Slot at top for standard badge clip/lanyard.
  Designed for conference/office wear.

  Overall Dimensions: 90mm tall x 60mm wide x 12mm deep
  Corner Radius:      4mm on all corners

  Key Changes from Standard Stack:
  1. Front wall: 1.2mm (reduced from 1.5mm -- still structural)
  2. Gap: 0.8mm (reduced from 2.5mm -- tight FPC fold)
  3. Battery: 4.4mm thin (custom LiPo, wider to compensate)
     - Standard 5mm battery won't fit
     - Custom cell: 40 x 50 x 4.4mm (~400mAh)
     - Or: 2x ultra-thin cells (2.2mm each) side by side
  4. No air gap between PCB and battery (direct contact OK with
     insulation tape)
  5. Back wall: 1.5mm (standard)

  Alternative: Accept 13mm depth

================================================================================
  MOUNTING & ASSEMBLY DETAILS
================================================================================

  Display Mounting:
  - Recessed pocket in front shell: 52.0 x 36.4mm
  - Pocket depth: 2.5mm
  - Display held by adhesive foam frame
  - Landscape orientation (long axis horizontal)
  - FPC folds tightly (0.8mm gap -- use flex-rated FPC)

  PCB Mounting:
  - 4x molded standoffs in back shell (0.8mm tall)
  - PCB sits on standoffs, screwed down with M2 screws
  - Standoffs set the gap between display and PCB
  - Components face back (toward battery)

  Battery Mounting:
  - Custom thin LiPo in molded pocket in back shell
  - Pocket walls prevent lateral movement
  - Foam pad on top (between battery and PCB)
  - JST connector, short lead

  Button Mounting:
  - 3 buttons along bottom edge, landscape layout
  - Standard tactile switches on PCB
  - Caps: 12 x 6mm, press-fit in shell
  - 14mm center-to-center spacing

  Wake Button:
  - Right side edge, tactile switch on PCB edge
  - Cap: 10 x 4.5mm, flush with side surface
  - Easily reachable with thumb when holding badge

  Lanyard Slot:
  - Molded as part of both shell halves
  - Front and back shells each have half the slot
  - When assembled, slot is complete
  - Internal reinforcement ribs for strength

  Shell Assembly:
  - Front and back shells snap together
  - Perimeter snap clips (6 points)
  - 2x M2 screws near bottom for security
  - Thin foam gasket around perimeter

  USB-C:
  - Bottom edge, centered
  - 9.34 x 3.66mm cutout
  - Accessible while wearing on lanyard

================================================================================
  SPECIAL FEATURES
================================================================================

  1. Lanyard Attachment: Standard badge slot at top
     - Compatible with all common lanyard hardware
     - Reinforced slot walls to handle swinging/tugging
     - Device hangs screen-forward on chest

  2. Ultra-Thin Profile: 12mm deep
     - Requires custom thin battery (4.4mm)
     - Tight FPC fold (0.8mm gap)
     - Slightly thinner front wall (1.2mm)
     - Comfortable for all-day wear

  3. Landscape Orientation:
     - Natural reading position when hanging from lanyard
     - Wider viewing area feels like a tiny monitor
     - Screen content laid out horizontally

  4. Conference Badge Aesthetic:
     - Looks like a smart conference badge
     - Professional appearance for office/events
     - Optional: clear back panel to show PCB (tech aesthetic)

  5. Flat Back:
     - No protrusions, lies flat against chest
     - Won't catch on clothing
     - Comfortable for extended wear

  6. Dual Wear Modes:
     - Lanyard: hang from neck for passive monitoring
     - Hand-held: grab and interact with buttons
     - Size comfortable for both modes

  7. Screen Auto-Rotate:
     - Accelerometer on PCB detects orientation
     - When picked up and rotated, screen adjusts
     - Lanyard mode: specific layout optimized for glance-reading

  8. Visibility:
     - Screen faces outward when worn
     - Colleagues can see your Devotchi's mood
     - Conversation starter at conferences
