DESIGN 6: HEX TOWER
====================
Vertical hexagonal prism. Screen, buttons, and speaker each get
their own dedicated facet. Standing tower form factor.
Think "desktop totem" or "smart speaker tower" aesthetic.

Overall: 80mm flat-to-flat hex, 120mm tall
Target user: desk centerpiece, premium feel

    The hex gives ~70mm internal depth from screen facet to back:
    Wall:           2mm
    Screen pocket:  5mm
    Gap:            3mm (ESP32 module clearance)
    PCB:            1.6mm
    Remaining:     ~58mm to opposite wall
    ────────────────────
    Plenty of room for speaker cavity behind PCB

COMPONENT PLACEMENT ON FACETS
─────────────────────────────

    Facet   Angle   Feature
    ─────   ─────   ───────
    1       30°     BUTTONS — 3x tactile, 12mm spacing, centered vertically
    2       90°     SCREEN — window 41.8x31.6mm, upper half of facet
    3       150°    Plain (decorative texture or logo)
    4       210°    SPEAKER — grille pattern, centered, lower half
    5       270°    USB-C — cutout near bottom (9.5x3.5mm)
    6       330°    Plain (decorative or vent slots)


COMPONENT LAYOUT
────────────────

    TOWER BODY (main hex shell, open top)
    ├── 6 facets, 2mm wall thickness
    ├── Screen pocket on facet 2 (inside face)
    │   ├── Pocket: 52.2 x 36.6mm, 5mm deep
    │   └── 4x retention clips
    ├── Button holes on facet 1 (3x, 5mm or 8mm dia)
    ├── Speaker grille on facet 4 (28mm dia pattern, 1.5mm holes)
    ├── USB-C cutout on facet 5 (9.5 x 3.5mm)
    ├── 4x PCB standoffs (from inner walls, M2)
    └── Speaker mounting shelf/pocket on inner wall of facet 4

    HEX CAP (top lid)
    ├── Hex plate, 2mm thick
    ├── Snap-fit tabs or 4x screw holes
    └── Slight chamfer on edges

    BOTTOM
    ├── Closed hex base (integrated with body or separate piece)
    ├── 6x rubber feet at hex vertices
    └── Optional: bottom access panel for USB-C cable routing


ASSEMBLY
────────
- 2-piece: tower body (open top) + hex cap
- Cap: snap-fit tabs (4x on alternating facets) or screws
- Screen, PCB, speaker all load from top before cap installed
- PCB screws to standoffs on inner walls
- Speaker press-fit with foam gasket against facet 4
- Cable exits bottom through slot or through facet 5 USB-C cutout
