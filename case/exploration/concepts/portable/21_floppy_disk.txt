DESIGN 21: FLOPPY DISK
======================
3.5" floppy disk tribute. Nearly square landscape form factor.
Screen hides behind a sliding metal shutter that slides sideways
to reveal the display — just like a real floppy's metal slider.
Label area on upper portion houses 3 buttons. Write-protect tab
hole in corner doubles as wake switch (slide tab). Authentic
floppy details: hub hole on center-back, notch on right edge.
Ultra-thin 10mm profile requires custom 3mm battery.

Overall: 90mm wide x 94mm tall x 10mm deep
Weight target: ~55g with battery
Orientation: Landscape (screen area)
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), 4x corner screws hidden under label
Battery: Custom slim LiPo 3mm thick (30x35mm, ~200mAh)

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, front to back)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT SHELL (floppy shape, 1.0mm wall)
    ├── Label area recess (top 35mm, 0.3mm deep for sticker)
    ├── 3x button holes in label area (12.5 x 6.5mm)
    ├── Shutter rail channel (0.5mm deep, full width)
    ├── Screen window cutout (42 x 32mm, below shutter)
    ├── Write-protect tab opening (bottom-right corner, 5x8mm)
    ├── USB-C cutout (bottom edge, 9.5 x 4.0mm)
    └── Hub hole cosmetic ring (back side, 15mm dia)

    Layer 2 — METAL SHUTTER ASSEMBLY
    ├── Stamped stainless steel shutter (50 x 35 x 0.3mm)
    ├── Spring-loaded return (small leaf spring)
    ├── Rides in 0.5mm rail channel in front shell
    └── Slides right to reveal screen, spring returns left

    Layer 3 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted landscape in lower section
    ├── FPC cable exits to PCB connector
    └── Held by 4x retention clips

    Layer 4 — AIR GAP (1.4mm)
    ├── FPC routing
    └── Button plunger passage from label area to PCB

    Layer 5 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 faces back
    ├── 3x tactile switches in upper zone (label button alignment)
    ├── 1x wake switch at bottom-right corner (write-protect tab)
    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge
    ├── Display FPC connector
    └── Vibration motor pads

    Layer 6 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 3mm custom slim LiPo)
    ├── ~200mAh capacity (reduced from standard 5mm)
    ├── Centered behind PCB
    └── JST pigtail to PCB

    Layer 7 — BACK SHELL (floppy shape, 1.0mm wall)
    ├── Hub hole cosmetic detail (15mm ring, raised 0.5mm)
    ├── Right-edge notch (authentic floppy detail)
    ├── Battery cradle ribs
    ├── 4x PCB mounting posts
    ├── USB-C cutout alignment
    └── 4x screw bosses (hidden under front label)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece floppy shape (front + back), 4x M2 screws
  hidden beneath the peel-on label sticker on front
- Shutter assembly installs into front shell rail before
  closing case — slides in from the right edge
- Spring return: small leaf spring in left pocket of rail
- Label is a thick vinyl sticker (0.3mm) that covers screw heads
  and provides authentic floppy label aesthetic
- Display press-fits into front shell pocket below shutter zone
- PCB mounts on 4x posts in back shell
- Custom 3mm LiPo required for 10mm target depth
- Hub hole on back is cosmetic only — raised ring detail
- Right-edge notch is cosmetic — matches real floppy geometry
- USB-C on bottom edge aligns with where floppy head slot would be
- Write-protect tab mechanism: small sliding plastic tab in corner
  with cam surface that depresses wake switch beneath

SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Sliding metal shutter reveals/hides screen — satisfying tactile click
- Authentic 3.5" floppy proportions (90x94mm vs real 90x93.7mm)
- Functional write-protect tab as wake/sleep toggle
- Peel-and-replace label area for customization
- Hub hole detail on back for authenticity
- Ultra-thin 10mm profile (thinner than real floppy's 9mm body
  when including shutter height)
- Conversation starter / nostalgia piece
- Shutter spring return provides haptic feedback
