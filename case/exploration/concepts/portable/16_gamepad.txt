DESIGN 16: GAMEPAD
==================
Dual-grip game controller shape. Landscape orientation.
Screen centered in the body. Left grip has D-pad style rocker
(approve=up, reject=down). Right grip has 2 round action buttons
(A/B style). Textured rubber grip surfaces on both horns.
Comfortable ergonomic two-hand hold. Trigger on back for wake.
Chunky, satisfying to hold. Think classic console controller.

Overall: 120mm wide x 60mm tall x 20mm deep
Weight target: ~75g with battery
Orientation: Landscape
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), 6x screw posts

    └── Clearance for ESP32 module height

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece gamepad shape (front + back), 6x M2 screws
- 4x screws in body section, 1x in each grip horn
- Seam runs around perimeter at mid-depth
- Display press-fits into front shell pocket, 4x retention clips
- PCB mounts on 4x posts in body section of back shell
- D-pad: Single-piece rocker sits on 5-dome switch PCBlet in left grip
- D-pad PCBlet connects to main PCB via short FPC ribbon
- Action buttons: Individual plungers on 2-switch PCBlet in right grip
- Action PCBlet connects to main PCB via short FPC ribbon
- Trigger: Single switch on small PCBlet, FPC to main PCB
- Battery in cradle ribs, centered in body, JST pigtail to PCB
- USB-C cutout on top edge center, aligned across both halves
- Vibration motor foam-taped inside back shell (body center)
- Rubber grip panels: Either TPU overmold (injection) or adhesive
  rubber sheets (3D print / prototype version)
- Grip horns are hollow — FPC ribbons route through internal channels
- Back shell has ergonomic palm contour (4.9mm extra depth)
- 6x screw counterbores on back are recessed flush


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Dual-grip ergonomics: Full two-hand controller feel. Left hand
  controls D-pad (approve/reject/navigate), right hand has A/B
  action buttons. Natural, intuitive input mapping.
- D-pad approve/reject: Up=approve (green), Down=reject (red).
  Directional input feels decisive and satisfying. Center click
  for select/confirm. Navigate menus with left/right.
- Back trigger wake: Index finger trigger on back of left grip.
  Natural reach, no fumbling. Also serves as modifier when awake.
- Textured rubber grips: Diamond crosshatch pattern on both horns.
  TPU overmold or adhesive rubber panels. Comfortable, non-slip
  grip for extended holding.
- Chunky satisfying form: 120mm wide controller body with 20mm
  depth. Substantial, premium hand-feel. The kind of device you
  pick up and don't want to put down.
- Ergonomic back contour: Back shell has 4.9mm of extra contouring
  beyond the z-stack minimum. Palm rests naturally on the curved
  surface. No sharp edges or flat spots.
- Top-edge USB-C: Charging cable exits upward, doesn't interfere
  with grip during use. Can charge while operating.
- Hollow grip horns: Internal FPC ribbon channels connect grip
  button PCBlets to main PCB. Clean routing, no external wires.
- Color scheme: Body in matte black, grip rubber in dark gray,
  button caps in classic SNES-style colors (A=blue, B=red,
  D-pad in dark gray with colored arrow markings).
