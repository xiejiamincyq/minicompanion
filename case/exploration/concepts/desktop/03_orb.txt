DESIGN 3: FLOATING ORB
======================
Egg-shaped head on a cylindrical pedestal base. Screen embedded
in the flat-cut top of the egg. Think "desk toy" or "smart speaker"
form factor. Two-piece egg + separate base.

Overall: ~80mm egg dia x ~55mm egg height, 65mm base dia x 40mm base height
Total height: ~110mm (base + peg + egg)
Target user: conversation piece, living room display

COMPONENT LAYOUT
────────────────

    EGG — FRONT HALF (top half of egg)
    ├── Flat-cut top face with screen window
    ├── Screen pocket (52.2 x 36.6mm, depth = module thickness)
    ├── 4x retention clips holding screen in pocket
    ├── Button holes on equator band (4x, 5mm dia)
    └── Shell wall: 2mm, spherical profile

    EGG — BACK HALF (bottom half of egg)
    ├── 4x PCB standoffs (5mm OD, M2, 3mm tall)
    ├── Peg socket (8mm hole, 10mm deep) for base connection
    ├── Wire channel from peg socket to PCB area
    └── Shell wall: 2mm

    PEDESTAL BASE
    ├── Cylinder: 65mm OD, 40mm tall
    ├── Peg on top (8mm dia, 15mm tall) — friction fit into egg
    ├── Speaker cavity inside (28mm dia, 6mm deep)
    ├── Speaker grille on side wall
    ├── USB-C cutout on side wall (9.5 x 3.5mm)
    ├── Wire channel from peg through to internal USB-C breakout
    └── 4x rubber feet on bottom


WIRING NOTES
────────────
- FPC from screen → PCB in egg (short, direct connection)
- USB-C on base connects via wire through hollow peg to PCB in egg
- Speaker in base connects via wire through hollow peg
- Peg is hollow (5mm bore) for wire routing
- Alternative: all electronics in base, only screen+FPC in egg


ASSEMBLY
────────
- Egg: 2 halves (horizontal split at equator), 4x M2 screws
- Base: 2 pieces (top plate + cylinder body), snap-fit or 3x screws from bottom
- Egg sits on base peg (friction fit, can lift off)
- Total 3 subassemblies
