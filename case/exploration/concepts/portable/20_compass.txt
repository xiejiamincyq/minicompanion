DESIGN 20: COMPASS (HUNTER-CASE)
=================================
Circular brass-aesthetic compass with a hinged hunter-case lid that flips
open 180 degrees to reveal the TFT screen. Lid exterior features an engraved
compass rose. Three buttons on the bezel ring. Lanyard ring at 12 o'clock,
USB-C at 6 o'clock. Lid protects screen when closed. Pocket watch meets
Tamagotchi — elegant, protective, tactile.

Overall: 60mm diameter x 18mm tall (closed)
Weight target: ~50g with battery
Orientation: Portrait (screen in circular frame)
Shell: 3-piece (base + bezel ring + hinged lid), hinge pin

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, lid to base bottom)
────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — HINGED LID (circular, 60mm dia, 2.0mm thick with walls)
    ├── Outer surface: compass rose engraving (0.3mm deep)
    ├── Inner surface: smooth, 0.5mm standoff bumps (protects screen)
    ├── Hinge barrel at 12 o'clock (2mm ID, accepts hinge pin)
    ├── Snap clasp at 6 o'clock (small leaf spring)
    ├── 1.0mm outer wall + 1.0mm inner cavity
    └── Brass-colored finish (anodized aluminum or painted resin)

    Layer 2 — BEZEL RING (circular, 60mm OD, 52mm ID, 1.5mm tall)
    ├── Sits at seam between lid and base
    ├── 3x button cutouts at 3, 6, 9 o'clock positions
    ├── Hinge pin boss at 12 o'clock (also holds lanyard ring)
    ├── Lid snap clasp receiver at 6 o'clock
    ├── Press-fits onto base shell
    └── Brass-colored to match lid

    Layer 3 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted portrait inside circular base cavity
    ├── Circular mask/bezel hides rectangular corners
    ├── Active area: ~40mm diameter visible circle
    ├── FPC cable exits toward 6 o'clock (bottom)
    └── Held by retention clips in base shell

    Layer 4 — 2.0mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC cable routes to PCB connector
    └── Button plungers route from bezel ring to PCB switches

    Layer 5 — PCB (45 x 40mm x 1.6mm)
    ├── Trimmed corners to fit within 52mm circular cavity
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 faces back (toward base bottom)
    ├── 3x tactile switches at 3, 6, 9 o'clock (face up toward bezel)
    ├── 1x wake switch at 6 o'clock (shared with or near Action button)
    ├── USB-C connector at 6 o'clock edge
    ├── Display FPC connector at 12 o'clock edge
    └── Mounted on posts from base shell

    Layer 6 — 0.4mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module height

    Layer 7 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo, 300-500mAh)
    ├── Centered in base cavity
    ├── JST connector to PCB (short pigtail)
    └── Held by cradle ribs in base shell

    Layer 8 — BASE SHELL (circular, 60mm dia, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── 4x PCB mounting posts (trimmed to fit circular interior)
    ├── Battery cradle ribs
    ├── Bezel ring press-fit groove at top edge
    ├── USB-C cutout at 6 o'clock (9.5 x 4.0mm)
    ├── Hinge pin boss at 12 o'clock
    └── Flat bottom (can stand on desk)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 3-piece (base + bezel ring + hinged lid) + hinge pin
- Base shell: circular, flat bottom, holds all internals
- Bezel ring: press-fits onto base shell top edge, holds button plungers
- Lid: hinged at 12 o'clock, snaps closed at 6 o'clock
- Hinge: 2mm steel pin through aligned barrels, spring detent at 0/180 deg
- Display press-fits into base cavity with circular mask overlay
- PCB has corners trimmed to fit within 52mm circular internal diameter
- PCB mounts on posts from base shell bottom
- Battery in cradle ribs within base shell
- USB-C at 6 o'clock — can charge while standing upright
- Lanyard ring integrated into hinge boss at 12 o'clock
- Brass aesthetic: anodized aluminum or brass-painted resin for lid + bezel
- Compass rose: CNC engraved or molded into lid exterior (0.3mm depth)
- Lid protects screen when closed — 0.5mm air gap prevents contact
- Three standoff bumps on lid interior prevent screen touching during shock
- Flat base bottom allows compass to sit on desk as a display stand
- When open, lid lies flat behind base — total footprint ~120mm long
- Wake: double-press Action button (no 4th button needed)
- Portrait screen orientation inside circular mask frame
