DESIGN 23: CYBERDECK
====================
Compact hacker terminal aesthetic. Landscape brick with an angled
top face (~15deg tilt) giving the screen a built-in viewing angle.
Front vertical face has 3 mechanical-style buttons with visible
square stems and RGB LED rings. Exposed hex screw heads on all
4 corners (functional). One side panel is transparent/clear acrylic
showing PCB edge and components inside. Industrial/utilitarian
aesthetic. Rubber feet on bottom. Sticker area on back.

Overall: 95mm wide x 60mm deep x 20mm tall (front) / 36mm tall (back)
Weight target: ~65g with battery
Orientation: Landscape (screen on angled top face)
Shell: Multi-piece (frame + clear side panel + top + bottom)
       4x M2.5 hex socket screws on corners

    Z-stack (vertical, at front face):
    1.5 (top wall) + 2.5 + 2.5 + 1.6 + 0.5 + 5.0 + 1.5 = 15.1mm
    Front height: ~20mm (15.1mm stack + button housing)
    Back height: ~36mm (15.1mm stack + angled rise of ~16mm
    over 60mm depth at 15deg)

    The 15deg tilt means the back rises:
    60mm x tan(15deg) = 60 x 0.268 = ~16mm above front


COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, top to bottom)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — TOP SHELL (angled face, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── 15deg angled surface for screen
    ├── Screen window cutout (42 x 32mm, landscape)
    ├── Integrated with front face (one piece)
    ├── 4x corner hex screw through-holes (M2.5)
    └── Screen retention clips (4x)

    Layer 2 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted landscape on 15deg angled shelf
    ├── FPC cable exits toward back
    └── Held by top shell clips

    Layer 3 — FRONT BUTTON PANEL (vertical face)
    ├── 3x mechanical button assemblies
    │   ├── Square plunger stems (visible 2mm travel)
    │   ├── RGB LED ring PCBs (3x, wired to main PCB)
    │   └── Tactile switches on main PCB behind
    └── Buttons fire horizontally into PCB switches

    Layer 4 — 2.5mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC routing (angled path to PCB)
    ├── Button stem passages
    └── LED wiring channel

    Layer 5 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 faces right side (visible through clear panel)
    ├── 3x tactile switches facing front
    ├── USB-C connector on back edge (centered)
    ├── Display FPC connector
    ├── 3x LED ring headers
    └── Vibration motor pads

    Layer 6 — 0.5mm GAP
    └── ESP32 module clearance

    Layer 7 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo, 300-500mAh)
    ├── Positioned in lower rear section
    ├── JST pigtail to PCB
    └── Held by bottom shell cradle

    Layer 8 — BOTTOM SHELL (1.5mm wall)
    ├── 4x rubber foot recesses (8mm dia)
    ├── Battery cradle ribs
    ├── 4x M2.5 threaded inserts (corners)
    ├── PCB mounting posts
    ├── USB-C cutout
    └── Battery access panel (snap-off, bottom center)

    SIDE PANELS
    ├── Right: Clear acrylic (2mm thick), press-fit into frame
    │   └── Shows PCB edge, ESP32, components, LED glow
    └── Left: Opaque shell material, integrated with frame

    LED ring states:
    ◯ = idle (dim white)
    ◯ = active (bright per-button color)
    ◯ = pressed (flash)


MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: Multi-piece construction
  - Main frame: top + front + left side + bottom (one molded piece
    or 3D printed as a unit)
  - Clear acrylic right side panel (2mm, laser-cut, slides into groove)
  - 4x M2.5 hex socket head cap screws hold it all together
- 15deg angled top face gives natural screen viewing angle
  when device sits on desk or is held
- Buttons on vertical front face have mechanical-style square stems
  with visible 2mm travel and audible click
- RGB LED rings around each button powered from main PCB
- Clear panel slides into frame groove, retained by corner screws
- Rubber feet (4x, 8mm dia, 2mm tall) prevent sliding on desk
- USB-C on back edge for clean cable routing on desk
- Back face has recessed label area for "property of" sticker
- Battery access via snap panel on bottom
- Vibration motor adhered to bottom shell interior
- Assembly order: PCB onto posts, battery in cradle, slide in
  clear panel, close top/front, insert 4x hex screws

SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Built-in 15deg screen tilt — no stand needed
- Visible mechanical button stems with RGB LED rings
- Transparent side panel shows internals (PCB, ESP32, glow)
- Exposed hex screw industrial aesthetic (all 4 corners)
- "Property of" label area on back (asset tag / hacker cred)
- Rubber feet for stable desk placement
- Compact hacker terminal / cyberdeck aesthetic
- Button LED rings provide per-button status indication
- Clear panel lets internal LED glow create ambient light effect
- Asymmetric design (one clear side, one opaque) adds visual interest
