DESIGN 1: CLASSIC EGG
=====================
Original Tamagotchi-inspired egg/oval shape. Portrait orientation.
Rounded top, flatter bottom. Screen in upper half, buttons below.
Think "1996 Bandai Tamagotchi" scaled up for a real TFT display.

Overall: 70mm tall x 55mm wide x 15.1mm deep
Weight target: ~45g with battery
Orientation: Portrait
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), snap-fit tabs

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, front to back)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT SHELL (egg-shaped, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── Screen window cutout (41.8 x 31.6mm, portrait)
    ├── 3x main button holes (12.5 x 6.5mm rectangular)
    ├── 1x wake button hole (10.5 x 5.0mm)
    ├── 1x LED hole (2mm dia, above screen)
    ├── 4x screen retention clips (L-shaped, 0.5mm overhang)
    ├── 4x snap-fit tab sockets (around perimeter)
    └── 1x lanyard hole boss (3mm ID, reinforced)

    Layer 2 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted portrait (long axis vertical)
    ├── Seated in pocket with 0.2mm clearance per side
    ├── FPC cable exits bottom edge of module
    └── Held by front shell retention clips

    Layer 3 — 2.5mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC cable routes down to PCB connector
    └── Button plungers pass through (front shell → PCB switches)

    Layer 4 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5mm) faces back
    ├── 4x tactile switches face front
    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge (centered)
    ├── Display FPC connector on top edge
    ├── Vibration motor pads near bottom
    └── Mounted on 4x posts from back shell (press-fit)

    Layer 5 — 0.5mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module height (2.4mm fits
        within combined gap + battery clearance)

    Layer 6 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo, 300-500mAh)
    ├── Centered in lower portion of egg
    ├── JST connector to PCB (short pigtail)
    └── Held by cradle ribs in back shell

    Layer 7 — BACK SHELL (egg-shaped, 1.5mm wall)
    ├── 4x PCB mounting posts (3mm OD, press-fit to PCB holes)
    ├── Battery cradle ribs (2x, 1mm tall)
    ├── 4x snap-fit tabs (mate with front shell sockets)
    ├── USB-C cutout (9.5 x 4.0mm)
    └── Optional: battery door (snap-off panel, 32 x 37mm)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece egg (front + back), 4x snap-fit tabs, no screws
- Seam runs around the egg perimeter at the widest point (mid-depth)
- Display press-fits into front shell pocket, held by 4x retention clips
- PCB press-fits onto 4x posts in back shell
- Battery sits in cradle ribs, JST pigtail connects to PCB
- USB-C cutout aligned at bottom center of both shell halves
- Vibration motor adhered to inside of back shell with foam tape
- Lanyard hole at top center, 3mm ID, reinforced boss
- Egg profile: elliptical cross-section, max 15.1mm at center,
  tapering to ~12mm at top/bottom extremes
- Portrait orientation: display rotated 90deg from landscape default
