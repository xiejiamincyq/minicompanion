DESIGN 24: HOLSTER
==================
Belt-mounted quick-draw pod with tactical/military aesthetic.
Screen on front face always visible when holstered on belt.
3 buttons on top edge for easy thumb access when reaching down.
Quick-release tab on one side. Body angled 15deg when worn for
easy glancing down. MOLLE-compatible belt loop. Drainage hole
on bottom. Kydex-style rigid holster look.

Overall: 70mm wide x 50mm tall x 16mm deep (device pod)
Belt loop: 40mm wide x 15mm tall (MOLLE-compatible strap)
Weight target: ~55g with battery
Orientation: Portrait (screen on front face)
Shell: 2-piece (front + back), 2x side screws + quick-release

QUICK-RELEASE MECHANISM DETAIL
───────────────────────────────

    Right side of pod:

    ┌──────────────────┐
    │                  │
    │      POD         │
    │                  │      ┌──────┐
    │              ────╫──────┤ QR   │ ← Spring-loaded tab
    │              ────╫──────┤ TAB  │   Push inward to release
    │                  │      └──────┘   from belt mount plate
    │                  │
    └──────────────────┘

    Belt mount plate (stays on belt):
    ┌────────────────┐
    │   ┌──────────┐ │
    │   │ SLOT     │ │ ← Pod slides down into slot
    │   │          │ │   QR tab clicks into detent
    │   │  ┌──┐   │ │
    │   │  │◄─│   │ │ ← Detent notch (QR tab engages here)
    │   │  └──┘   │ │
    │   └──────────┘ │
    └────────────────┘

    Operation:
    1. Slide pod down into belt mount plate
    2. QR tab clicks into detent — locked
    3. Push QR tab inward — tab retracts, pod lifts free

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece pod (front + back), 2x M2 side screws
- Quick-release tab on right side (spring-loaded, push to detach)
- Belt mount plate is a separate piece that stays on belt
- Pod slides into mount plate and QR tab locks it
- 15deg forward tilt from offset strap attachment point
- Buttons on top edge — thumb-accessible when reaching down
- Screen always visible when holstered (portrait, facing outward)
- USB-C on bottom edge — cable hangs down naturally
- 2mm drainage hole on bottom prevents water/dust accumulation
- Kydex-style aesthetic: angular edges, raised ridges, matte
  textured surfaces, minimal radii on corners
- Vibration motor provides haptic alerts through belt contact
- MOLLE-compatible strap (40mm) works with tactical belts,
  standard belts (up to 50mm), and MOLLE webbing
- Wake button on lower front face, slightly recessed

SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Belt-mounted always-visible display — glanceable status
- Quick-release mechanism for fast detach/attach
- 15deg forward tilt for easy downward glancing
- Top-edge buttons for natural thumb access at belt level
- MOLLE-compatible strap works with tactical and standard belts
- Drainage hole for outdoor/weather use
- Kydex-style tactical/military aesthetic
- Vibration alerts felt through belt
- Separate belt mount plate — pod is usable standalone
- Quick-draw: grab pod, push QR tab, pod is free in hand
