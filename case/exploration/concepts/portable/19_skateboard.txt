DESIGN 19: SKATEBOARD / FINGERBOARD
=====================================
Very elongated landscape slab shaped like a miniature skateboard. Screen sits
flush on the flat deck top. Nose and tail kick up at gentle 15-degree angles.
Four 8mm spinning wheels on truck assemblies underneath. Grip tape texture
on side rails. Three tiny buttons clustered on the kicktail end.
Playful desk toy that also happens to be a Devotchi.

Overall: 96mm long x 26mm wide x 14mm tall (at deck, 18mm at wheels)
Weight target: ~42g with battery
Orientation: Landscape
Shell: 3-piece (deck top + deck base + 2x truck clips), snap-fit

COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, top to bottom)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — DECK TOP SHELL (1.2mm wall, elongated skateboard outline)
    ├── Screen window cutout (42 x 31mm, landscape)
    ├── 3x button holes on kicktail (6.5 x 4.5mm each)
    ├── Grip tape texture molded on side rails (0.3mm raised bumps)
    ├── Nose kick: 15-deg upturn, last 12mm of nose
    ├── Tail kick: 15-deg upturn, last 10mm of tail
    ├── 4x screen retention clips (L-shaped)
    └── 6x snap-fit tab sockets along perimeter

    Layer 2 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted landscape (long axis along deck length)
    ├── Seated in recessed pocket, offset toward nose
    ├── FPC cable exits toward center of deck
    └── Held by front shell retention clips

    Layer 3 — 1.3mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC cable routes to PCB connector
    └── Button plungers pass through at tail end

    Layer 4 — PCB (45 x 40mm x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 faces bottom (toward trucks)
    ├── 3x tactile switches at tail end, face top
    ├── 1x wake button on right edge (accessible from rail)
    ├── USB-C connector on nose end (centered)
    ├── Display FPC connector centered
    └── Mounted on posts from deck base shell

    Layer 5 — 0.4mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module underside components

    Layer 6 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 5mm LiPo, 300-500mAh)
    ├── Centered under PCB in main deck area
    ├── JST connector to PCB (short pigtail)
    └── Held by cradle ribs in deck base

    Layer 7 — DECK BASE SHELL (1.0mm wall)
    ├── 4x PCB mounting posts
    ├── Battery cradle ribs
    ├── 2x truck mounting bosses (M2 threaded inserts)
    ├── USB-C cutout at nose end
    └── 6x snap-fit tabs (mate with deck top)

    Layer 8 — TRUCK ASSEMBLIES (2x, clip-on)
    ├── Front truck: 18mm wide, 2x 8mm wheels on axle
    ├── Rear truck: 18mm wide, 2x 8mm wheels on axle
    ├── Trucks attach via M2 screws into deck base bosses
    ├── Wheels: 8mm diameter, 5mm wide, free-spinning on axle
    └── Axle: 1.5mm steel pin, press-fit into truck hangers

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 3-piece (deck top + deck base + 2x truck clips)
- Deck top and base snap together with 6x snap-fit tabs along rails
- Seam runs along the side rails at mid-height of the deck
- Display press-fits into deck top pocket, landscape orientation
- PCB press-fits onto posts in deck base
- Battery in cradle ribs within deck base
- Trucks attach via M2 screws from outside deck base into threaded inserts
- Wheels: 4x 8mm, free-spinning on 1.5mm steel axle pins
- USB-C accessible under nose — angled down by nose kick for easy cable plug
- Wake button on right rail — small tactile switch, flush with rail surface
- Grip tape texture: molded bumps on both rails for grip, 0.3mm height
- Nose/tail kicks are solid — no internal components in curved sections
- Landscape screen orientation matches the elongated deck shape
- Very elongated profile (96x26mm) — fits in palm like a fingerboard
- Desk toy functionality: wheels spin, can roll on flat surfaces
