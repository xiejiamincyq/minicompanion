DESCRIPTION
───────────
Mini arcade machine desktop companion. Portrait-oriented screen sits behind
an arcade-style bezel tilted back ~15 degrees. Below the screen, a control
panel shelf angled at 20 degrees holds 3 oversized dome buttons and a
decorative joystick cap. A marquee header area at top provides space for an
LED strip or printed label. Speaker fires through a side vent panel. Coin
slot decoration on the right side. T-molding trim on cabinet edges. Weighted
base ensures tip-over resistance.

MOUNTING DETAILS
────────────────

  Screen Mount:
    - Portrait orientation in bezel opening (64.8×108mm visible)
    - 2 mm bezel lip on all sides
    - Tilted back 15° via angled internal rails
    - Held by 4 snap clips on module edges

  PCB Mount:
    - Angled shelf at 20° below screen
    - 4 × M2 brass standoffs (5mm height)
    - PCB sits component-side up (buttons face user)

  Dome Buttons:
    - 3 × 20mm dome caps press onto tactile switches via plungers
    - Through-hole mount in control panel face
    - Spacing: 25mm center-to-center

  Speaker:
    - Mounted internally behind left side vent panel
    - Vent: horizontal slat pattern (8 slats, 1.5mm wide)
    - Sealed chamber behind speaker for better acoustics

  USB-C:
    - Cutout on flat back panel, centered horizontally
    - 8.94 × 3.26 mm opening
    - 15mm from bottom edge

  Weight:
    - Steel disc or plate, press-fit into base cavity
    - Target: 80-100g for tip-over resistance


ASSEMBLY NOTES
──────────────

  1. Insert steel weight into base cavity, press-fit or adhesive
  2. Attach rubber feet to base bottom (4 corners)
  3. Mount PCB onto angled shelf standoffs (4 × M2 screws)
  4. Route USB-C extension to back panel cutout
  5. Install speaker in side chamber, route wires to PCB
  6. Connect screen FPC to PCB, seat screen in bezel clips
  7. Install dome button caps through control panel holes
  8. Snap joystick decorative cap into place
  9. Insert marquee label or LED strip into header slot
  10. Join front shell to back shell (4 × snap clips + 2 screws)
  11. Apply T-molding trim strips to cabinet edges

  Shell: 2-part (front + back), snap-fit with 2 security screws
  Material: PLA/PETG, black with optional side-art decals
  Print orientation: Front shell face-down, back shell face-down


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────

  T-Molding Trim:
    - Decorative edge trim channel on all vertical cabinet edges
    - Accepts standard 3/4" T-molding or 3D-printed inserts
    - Colors: neon pink, electric blue, or classic black

  Coin Slot Decoration:
    - Right side panel: embossed/recessed coin slot shape
    - Non-functional, purely aesthetic
    - Can be painted metallic silver for realism

  Marquee Header:
    - Translucent panel at top (2mm frosted acrylic or PETG)
    - Backlit by optional 5× WS2812B LED strip
    - Removable/swappable label insert slot
    - Default: "DEVOTCHI" retro pixel font

  Joystick Cap:
    - Decorative ball-top cap (15mm diameter)
    - Mounted on short fixed post
    - Does not move — purely cosmetic
    - Classic red or black ball top

  Weighted Base:
    - Steel plate counterbalances top-heavy cabinet
    - Low center of gravity prevents tip-over
    - Base slightly wider than cabinet body (100mm full width)

  Retro Aesthetic:
    - Side art panel areas for custom vinyl decals
    - Optional "Player 1" / "Insert Coin" text embossing
    - Control panel can be printed in contrasting color
