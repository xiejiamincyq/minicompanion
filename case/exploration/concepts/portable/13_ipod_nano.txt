DESIGN 13: iPOD NANO
====================
Click-wheel era Apple iPod tribute. Very slim tall portrait form.
Screen dominates the upper 60% of the front face. Below: a circular
click-wheel ring with center approve button. Reject and action buttons
at 12 and 6 o'clock on the wheel rim. Wake button and hold switch on
top edge. Rounded back with slight dome. Polished aluminum aesthetic.
Extremely thin — uses custom slim battery layout to hit 14mm depth.

Overall: 88mm tall x 42mm wide x 14mm deep
Weight target: ~38g with battery
Orientation: Portrait
Shell: 2-piece (front plate + back shell), 4x M2 screws hidden under back

    Click-wheel specs:
    - 30mm outer diameter ring
    - 15mm inner diameter (center button area)
    - Ring width: 7.5mm
    - Raised tactile ridge on ring surface (0.3mm, concentric grooves)
    - Ring is decorative/tactile — NOT a touch surface
    - Center button: 15mm diameter, 0.5mm raised, convex surface
    - 12 o'clock button: REJECT (12x6mm cap, embedded in ring top)
    - 6 o'clock button: ACTION (12x6mm cap, embedded in ring bottom)
    - 3 and 9 o'clock: smooth ring only (no buttons)
    - All buttons are standard tactile switches underneath


COMPONENT LAYOUT (exploded, front to back)
──────────────────────────────────────────

    Layer 1 — FRONT PLATE (88 x 42mm, 1.0mm, aluminum-look)
    ├── Screen window cutout (42.8 x 32.6mm, portrait)
    ├── Click-wheel aperture (31mm dia circular cutout)
    ├── Center button hole (16mm dia, within wheel aperture)
    ├── 2x button slots in wheel ring (12 o'clock + 6 o'clock)
    ├── LED hole (2mm dia, below wheel area)
    ├── 4x screen retention clips (L-shaped, 0.5mm overhang)
    ├── Polished/brushed aluminum finish (or anodized plastic)
    └── Edges chamfered 0.3mm at 45° for premium feel

    Layer 2 — CLICK-WHEEL ASSEMBLY (snap-in subassembly)
    ├── Wheel ring (30mm OD, 15mm ID, 2mm thick)
    │   ├── Decorative concentric grooves (tactile ridge)
    │   ├── 2x button cap recesses (12 + 6 o'clock positions)
    │   └── Snaps into front plate aperture
    ├── Center button cap (15mm dia, convex, 0.5mm proud)
    │   └── Sits on tactile switch plunger
    └── 2x ring button caps (12x6mm, sit on tactile switch plungers)

    Layer 3 — DISPLAY MODULE (51.8 x 36.2 x 2.5mm)
    ├── Mounted portrait (long axis vertical)
    ├── Upper 60% of internal volume
    ├── FPC cable exits bottom edge toward PCB
    └── Held by front plate retention clips

    Layer 4 — 2.5mm AIR GAP
    ├── FPC cable routes from display to PCB connector
    ├── Button plungers pass through (3x from wheel area)
    └── Wake button and hold switch actuator rods

    Layer 5 — PCB (45 x 40 x 1.6mm)
    ├── ESP32-S3-MINI-1 (15.4 x 20.5mm) faces back
    ├── 3x main tactile switches face front (triangular layout)
    │   ├── Center: approve (under wheel center)
    │   ├── Top: reject (under 12 o'clock position)
    │   └── Bottom: action (under 6 o'clock position)
    ├── 1x wake tactile switch on top edge
    ├── 1x hold slide switch on top edge
    ├── USB-C connector on bottom edge (centered)
    ├── Display FPC connector on upper area
    ├── Vibration motor pads near bottom
    └── Mounted on 4x posts from back shell

    Layer 6 — 0.5mm GAP
    └── Clearance for ESP32 module (2.4mm total with battery gap)

    Layer 7 — BATTERY (30 x 35 x 4.5mm slim LiPo, 250-350mAh)
    ├── Centered in lower portion
    ├── Slim cell selected for 14mm total depth target
    ├── JST connector to PCB (short pigtail)
    └── Held by cradle ribs in back shell

    Layer 8 — BACK SHELL (88 x 42mm, 1.4-1.5mm wall, domed)
    ├── Gentle dome (1.5mm crown over 42mm width)
    ├── 4x PCB mounting posts (3mm OD, press-fit)
    ├── Battery cradle ribs (2x, 1mm tall)
    ├── Battery door (snap-off panel, 32 x 37mm)
    ├── 4x M2 screw holes (hidden, countersunk)
    ├── USB-C cutout (9.5 x 4.0mm)
    ├── Vibration motor pocket (7mm dia, foam tape)
    └── Polished aluminum-look finish (matching front)

MOUNTING / ASSEMBLY NOTES
─────────────────────────
- Shell: 2-piece — flat front plate + domed back shell
- Front plate: 1.0mm aluminum (or rigid aluminum-look plastic)
- Back shell: 1.4-1.5mm domed plastic with aluminum finish
- 4x M2 screws from back (countersunk, hidden under battery door)
- Click-wheel ring snaps into front plate circular aperture
- Display press-fits into front plate pocket, held by retention clips
- PCB press-fits onto 4x posts in back shell
- Battery sits in cradle ribs, JST pigtail connects to PCB
- USB-C cutout at bottom center of back shell
- Vibration motor adhered inside back shell with foam tape
- Hold switch and wake button mounted on PCB top edge
- Actuator rods extend from PCB switches through gap to top edge caps
- Front plate edges: 0.3mm chamfer at 45° all around for premium feel
- Portrait orientation: display rotated 90° from landscape default
- Slim battery (4.5mm) trades capacity for thinness (~250-350mAh)
- Back dome provides comfortable grip and visual depth


SPECIAL FEATURES
────────────────
- Click-wheel aesthetic instantly recognizable as iPod homage
- Extremely slim profile (14mm) — thinnest concept in the lineup
- Hold switch prevents accidental button presses in pocket
- Polished aluminum-look finish on both front and back surfaces
- Domed back sits naturally in the palm
- Front plate chamfered edges catch light like real aluminum
- Center approve button is the largest and most prominent control
- Wheel ring tactile grooves provide fidget-friendly texture
- Screen dominates the front — 60% of face is display
- Minimalist aesthetic — only essential elements visible on front
